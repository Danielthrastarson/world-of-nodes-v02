Your command vocabulary

1–5 activate pinned instructions. Select people, press a key, then click the map. Shift-click adds route waypoints for MOVE. Ctrl-drag assigns an area for HOLD, OBSERVE or ENGAGE. Esc cancels target selection.

Alt+1–5 recalls troop groups. Ctrl+Alt+1–5 assigns the current selection. Drag to select; Shift adds. The group buttons provide the same controls; Ctrl-click a group button to assign it. Initial groups are line, ranged, light, couriers, and carriers.

Each general provides live local vision. Staffed outposts need an unobstructed signal path. Faded contacts are old reports, not current locations. Choosing a camera position reveals nothing.

Nearby orders take a short exchange. Distant orders need an available courier at the command position. An accepted input is not a delivered order. Keep couriers near a general or headquarters.

Supply carriers repeatedly haul food to their assigned rendezvous. Troops must meet them there. Reserve a carrier for each forward outpost: its staff also need food. Construction uses materials carried from headquarters and actual worker time. Select workers near headquarters, then click a hill site and Build.

Capture the enemy headquarters with five combatants and no defenders for thirty seconds. The match draws at twenty minutes. General replacement costs an existing person, ten food and one minute.

Try moving your light formation toward the bridge, keeping another force near the ford, and watching how your opponent responds. Your two scouts begin observing the approaches automatically.
