## Scope and intervention

180 final runs: three registered configurations × two arms × 30 paired seeds,72h. Each world contains18 embodied Persons, two inhabited settlements, a sovereign and locally appointed steward, six cultivation units, two caregiver/dependent households, existing B2 relationships and accelerated B3 maturation, voluntary military service, a seeded three-Person raid, paths, a buildable public crossing and a physically carried local report.

The only treatment is one local `CommissionCrossing` by the physically present steward at0h versus leaving the project pending. Both arms receive the same lawful initial appointment, report purpose, controlled six-hour steward deliberation hold, voluntary muster request and research-injected local protection objective at1h05m. All other Persons deliberate normally. The fixed hold does not freeze physiology or establish an office-residence obligation. Map familiarity is explicitly seeded prior observation without current remote stocks, people or boards.

The target is200 physical communal food quanta; each raider may steal at most10 through real work and transport. The crossing requires20 wood and8 nominal construction-hours. Actual labor depends on existing physiology. There are no new policy coefficients, social obligations, ownership rules, transport priority or free assets.

## What actually caused the changes

The representative alternate-route trace is:

local steward commission
→ existing project becomes legally eligible
→ Person2 completes its separate courier task
→ that same Person independently becomes the sole donor/builder
→ construction consumes actual material and labor
→ crossing opens
→ several Persons use the changed connectivity
→ production, consumption, residence and family outcomes differ.

The treatment does **not** show that the steward's received report ordered construction. Report arrival is5.084h in both arms of the alternate/scarce cases and24h in both long-detour arms. The report is a shared background capability, not the manipulated cause. The courier's later work is ordinary Person arbitration, not a scripted “courier becomes builder” instruction.

Active administration averages6.054→43.165h in the alternate pair and6.054→8.784h in the scarce pair; both long arms have72h. These are derived from the same living steward's actual presence, not a territorial bonus. The fixed initial hold contributes six hours to both arms. More administrative presence is not itself evidence of better governance or nourishment.

Both initially dependent Persons mature through B3. Care-food transfer stays0 in every arm; family membership does not guarantee support. Later deaths and locally learned losses activate existing B2 appraisals. Do not call a Person a biological child at death merely because it belonged to the initial-dependent measurement cohort.

## Security remains a separate outcome

Four physically assembled members receive the initial local protection objective in every run. Volunteering totals are7 per run on the shorter map and8 on the long map; only4 and5 respectively ever assemble. No new order is broadcast to absent or late members.

There are no hits or combat engagements in these integrated fixtures, and cumulative theft reaches30 quanta in every arm. This commission comparison establishes no additional prevented loss. It does not overturn F11C's controlled protection result and is not a replacement no-defense comparison.

Timing matters: in the alternate seed-zero trace raiders first steal12 quanta during4.083–4.333h, defenders reach the stock at5.096h, and the initial service term ends at12.017h. Construction completes14.461h. Raiders take the remaining18 during14.779–15.335h. It would be false to describe the whole raid as finished before the crossing opened. The later theft occurs after active service has ended. No service extension or mandatory replacement is added to rescue protection.

## Gate and stopping point

The safe integration gate passes: existing systems compose, the one local institutional intervention has traceable effects, and failures remain physical/behavioral rather than being hidden by a new system. A full ruler-controlled vertical slice is **not** passed.

The next unresolved decision concerns the player's relationship to embodied officials. Existing appointment assigns an office; it does not command residence, courier labor, project execution or military obedience. The research unit-order API has no sovereign issuer contract. Factual report delivery cannot supply those missing rights.

Recommended owner choice: test one **advisory civil project request** from the embodied sovereign to a steward, with physical delivery and voluntary acceptance/deferral/refusal. This recommendation is not implemented. Local-Crown-only controls and limited commands under explicit voluntary-service consent are alternatives documented in the decision note. No money, taxation, conscription, requisition, UI, conquest or new family institution is added.
