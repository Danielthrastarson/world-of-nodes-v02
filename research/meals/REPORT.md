# F0 mechanical adoption and conditional meals

The six proven mechanical corrections are adopted in `Current`. Conditional meals
remain an explicit research arm. The six-hour margin, institutions, information,
FIFO, physical parameters and ordinary reactive priorities are unchanged.

Specification: `docs/CONDITIONAL_MEALS.md`; starting revision33dc4b1, implementation

is overwritten. The final dataset is `live/` and `offline/`.

## Validation and limits

540 no-meal +540 conditional-meal frozen-belief composite/prefilter comparisons
match kernel elapsed time, nutrition, fatigue, starvation and delivered quantity.
The previous130 primitive contracts remain covered by the suite. Nine historical
scenarios reproduce their exact event/state hashes. Adopted Current and the previously
validated CoherentForecast agree at1,387 hourly checkpoints across19 scenarios.
Reason text/event strings differ from legacy although baseline physical outcomes
match. This is a covered contract result, not proof of perfect forecasts in changing
worlds. No hidden current queues, future arrivals or food updates enter predictions.

The minimal belief/goal/undertaking/action vocabulary is a typed, read-only view of
existing memory, route goal, accepted Job and kernel Action. No stronger commitment
or BDI/GOAP architecture is adopted. Tests exercise job/action separation, explicit
cancellation, and identical decisions from identical personal histories/RNG despite
changed hidden truth. See `docs/DECISION_FOUNDATION.md`.

## Trace: the first break moved into board-approach execution

Seed0 agent0 eats its personal buffer at5.016667h, then chooses C at5.033333h with
17.966667h nutrition. It enters F→X at6h and reaches X at8h with15h nutrition.
The locally updated queue beliefs make the full claim/acquire/return plan predict
consuming all10 acquired quanta. It chooses self-provisioning at F. At8.016667h a
subsequent conditional evaluation passes and it joins X→C. At8.566667h that forecast
again rejects the plan; it leaves the C-bound queue and seeks F, eventually admitted
back toward F at14.986667h and arriving18.85h.

This is not a failed accepted delivery and not starvation-priority activation at
X: nutrition remains above12h at the first reversal. The actual agent has no job or
communal cargo yet. Accepted-job leases therefore cannot explain it. The unchanged
reactive hierarchy reconsiders the whole undertaking and falls back to a personal
buffer trip when projected deliverable cargo vanishes. Believed queue costs and
physical congestion interact with that arbitration. The study does not identify
all failures as a single commitment defect or show that the conditional tape would
succeed under hidden real demand.

## Interpretation and gate

Planning conditional legal meals changes the first break from no accepted forecast
to incomplete board approach. It does not produce provisioning and worsens outcomes
relative to migration under the adopted no-meal control: four fewer survivors,
48 more starvation-person-hours,192 fewer quanta consumed and192 more spoiled.
No stronger commitment, lower margin, information improvement or transport priority
is adopted. Survival is not adequate provisioning: the starvation grace/reset
mechanics remain unchanged and all endpoint survivors have zero nutrition.

F0 can pass on mechanical conformance, bounded knowledge, explicit existing
undertakings/interruption and invariant preservation. Its gate does not require
hauling success. The next permitted work is F1 geography, using Current/no-meal.
