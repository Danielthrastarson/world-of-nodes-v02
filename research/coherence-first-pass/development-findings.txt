Initial static validation found a research comparator discrepancy: the old belief oracle advanced its world clock through the entire stipulated queue wait even after the focal agent died. The corrected forecast stopped at the focal death boundary. The new belief_endpoint oracle stops the wait at death; the original belief oracle remains unchanged for historical reproduction. Example: forecast terminal time 86,400,000ms; original oracle world time 103,760,010ms, focal already dead. This was a comparator-endpoint issue, not an autonomous-policy intervention. Static validation was rerun and passed before the live phase.
