No welfare conclusion follows from survival, consumption or receipt alone. Reduced starvation may coexist with displaced work; changed consumption is not proof of nourishment. Missing report age means no received report. `summary.json` retains all per-arm statistics, paired metrics, missing values and reproducibility hashes; `paired.csv` retains each seed's deltas. Runtime is excluded from causal differences.
