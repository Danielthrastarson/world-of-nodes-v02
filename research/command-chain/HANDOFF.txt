NEGATIVE RESULTS

Long-distance couriers reach the location at 19.075h but eat and sleep before
handoff. The revocation courier arrives at 21.325h but likewise fails to transmit.
Physical arrival is not communication. No sleep, food or transport rule was tuned.
Live late/stale/revocation-receipt effects therefore remain unexposed in those runs.

Separate physical-contact controls validate expired receipt, stale-but-valid
execution and delayed revocation. Withdrawal stops an open pending duty only
after receipt; it cannot undo an already completed commission. These are controlled
mechanism witnesses, not autonomous messenger successes.

Royal issuance, office acceptance, initial courier purpose and the refusal case
are controlled inputs. No autonomous government or general refusal psychology is
claimed. Orders create obligations; they do not force the next action or outcome.
