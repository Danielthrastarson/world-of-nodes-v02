# F13B — authority creates obligations, not outcomes

The approved office-based command institution produces the complete headless
chain: sovereign intent → physical message → accepted office → role obligation
→ Person decision → lawful local commission → voluntary construction → changed
movement and household outcomes. It does not guarantee delivery, compliance,
construction or improved welfare.

Primary source: `ff7052773c97be2d202dbb0b1e7a9bb3a03d2f6e` (runtime implementation
in `b1e2642`). Baseline: `f1abb74`. The frozen panel has **600 runs: ten cases,
two arms, thirty paired seeds**. Mechanism worlds run 24h; integration runs 72h.
All 600 final audits pass with zero FIFO overtakes. The displayed outcomes are
constant across these thirty seeds; this is reproducibility across seeded runs,
not thirty independent ecological environments or a population-level estimate.

**F13B and F13 pass at the registered headless command-chain scope.** The gate
decision and historical replay review are recorded separately in
[validation.json](validation.json). No previous research output is overwritten.

## Institution and implementation

The owner explicitly authorized this new institution. It is not a mechanical bug
fix. `Config.commands` enables accepted steward duties and requires existing
authority and information mechanisms. Historical configurations keep it disabled.
An appointment offer alone grants no new duty: the candidate accepts locally,
with the appointing sovereign physically present. The person and sovereign retain
the accepted office certificate. The primary experiments control this consensual
acceptance in both arms. Ordinary safe-idle acceptance is available through the
common Person policy, under documented provisional thresholds.

The sole executable order is `CommissionCrossing { project, settlement }` for the
recipient's assigned office. A project-site description must be personally known
to the issuer. It provides neither current remote stock nor guaranteed usefulness.
Orders and revocations are immutable typed information carried by actual Persons;
issuance creates no distant recipient state. Recipient validation uses the received
message, accepted office, own clock and personally received revocations. No remote
sovereign-health, current-material or global-withdrawal query supplies knowledge.

The common Person selector arbitrates the resulting duty. Eating, bodily
constraints, critical survival/recovery and already indivisible gathering remain
real. Eligible role obligations precede ordinary optional goals. Unknown routes or
survival constraints pause the undertaking rather than becoming refusal. Terminal
or paused duties release only their attributed pending queue application; movement
already underway remains physical. Explicit refusal is possible without punishment.

**Completed means the local commission was authorized.** It does not mean the
crossing was built. The existing project still needs voluntarily committed goods
and labor. Withdrawal cannot undo a completed commission or work already performed;
revoking the independent public project is outside this command's scope.

See [the authority contract](../../docs/COMMAND_AUTHORITY.md),
[registered experiment](../../docs/COMMAND_CHAIN_EXPERIMENT.md),
`src/provisioning/commands.rs`, its `adapter.rs`, and `command_research.rs`.
No taxes, seizure, conscription, compulsory labor, punishment, arbitrary citizen
orders, currency, obedience statistic or global command radius was added.

## What was controlled

The sovereign's choice to issue, office acceptance and willing courier purpose are
controlled inputs. This is not autonomous government formation or messenger
recruitment. The courier has a fixed rendezvous and personal route memory, not a
tracker for the absent recipient. The physical route and survival actions use the
ordinary kernel. Both paired arms carry the same factual report; treatment adds
the scoped order. A receipt test checks matched physical and factual-belief inputs
before the recipient's role choice.

Small fixtures hold recipient deliberation, but not physiology, until receipt or
20h. Integration retains F13A's fixed 6h steward release. These research controls
are disclosed in registration and per-run parameters, not new residence policies.
Refusal is an explicit controlled personal choice after valid receipt, not a
measured autonomous dissent rate. Source-map initial memories deliberately omit
food facts; the stale primary case does not establish a royal food expectation.

The first nine cases use four Persons and a crossing costing 20 wood plus 8 nominal
labor-hours. Local wood is 100, except the resource case's 5. Short message edges
are 0.25h; long ones are 8h before ordinary impairment. The integrated case reuses
the F13A alternate-route world, with its production, households, military, raid,
authority, paths and original F12 report. No quantities or priorities were tuned
after negative delivery outcomes appeared.

## Arrival is not communication

In distant, late and stale cases, courier 2 physically arrives at **19.075h**,
with 4.925h nutrition. It consumes six personal food quanta, then sleeps with
23.083333h fatigue. No order handoff occurs by 24h. The late deadline is 9.016667h;
in the stale world 120 source-food quanta spoil at 10h. These conditions happen,
but no live post-receipt effect can be inferred without receipt.

In the revocation case, the steward reaches its settlement at 19.075h, pauses for
critical hunger/fatigue and never commissions. Revocation carrier **3** arrives at
21.325h, eats and sleeps without handing over. The duty expires because its own
known deadline reaches 24h, not because the king's withdrawal was remotely known.
The primary `courier_arrival_h` metric concerns courier 2; it does not report every
carrier. The causal trace disambiguates this.

No faster handoff, extra food, changed sleep priority or telepathic cancellation was
added to remove these failures. [Exact event excerpts](TRACE_EXCERPTS.md) preserve
all ten paired seed-0 cases, including message, action, resource and time IDs.

## Separate receipt witnesses

Three deterministic [mechanism controls](MECHANISM_CONTROLS.md) cover the branches
not naturally exposed by the primary panel:

1. A courier physically delivers an expired order. The recipient rejects it; no
   active duty appears.
2. Eight source-food quanta spoil to physical waste during a real one-hour journey.
   A stale-but-valid instruction arrives; the normal Person selector undertakes
   return and local commission without gaining remote freshness information.
3. A steward begins a one-slot traversal at 0h; withdrawal is sent at 0.25h. The
   courier waits under FIFO, while the steward commissions at 1h. The courier
   reaches the steward and delivers withdrawal at 2h. Already completed commission
   remains; no retroactive removal of the action occurs.

Additional adversarial tests verify received withdrawal cancels a pending role
queue, unknown withdrawal changes no choice, early withdrawal prevents revival by
a later order, and expiration cannot teleport an underway traveler. A known
30-second deadline cancels the old role queue before a slot releases at 45 seconds,
even with the next ordinary policy tick at 60 seconds.

These controls demonstrate mechanics under deliberately arranged contact. They
are not counted as successful autonomous message delivery in the 600-run panel.

## Validation and reproducibility

The full suite has **339 passing tests**, including 29 command tests and three
command-research tests. Strict Clippy and formatting checks pass. New tests cover
scope, voluntary acceptance, information locality, personal refusal, survival,
deadline/revocation ordering, journal independence and irregular clock partitions.
All **17,880 primary hourly checkpoints** pass physical audits and FIFO checks.
Frozen runtime source hashes still match the primary provenance manifest.

An integrated clock-partition test exposed a preexisting military scheduling
defect before any F13 order: volunteering left a post-choice wake, letting an
arbitrary caller checkpoint advance the next enlistment action. The narrow repair
removes that wake. Failed diagnostic witnesses remain in

threshold, physiology, material, transport capacity or social coefficient changed.

## Scope of the conclusion

The missing institutional link is demonstrated: the ruler can create a scoped
obligation through physical information, and a Person can realize it through the
existing world rather than being directly controlled. Authorization and obligation
remain separate from successful physical provision.

Unresolved work includes autonomous courier recruitment, reliable post-arrival
handoff, autonomous refusal motives and political choice under uncertain reports.
None is silently repaired here. F14 collective political coordination, final UI,
new coercive powers and further economic institutions are not implemented.
