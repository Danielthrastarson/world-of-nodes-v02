# F10 — embodied local authority

**PASSED at the registered foundation scope.** A polity, a sovereign person and a
steward office are separate identities. Physical appointment creates a persistent
institutional record; actual local administration depends on a living holder's
presence. Territory is a derived set, never a border polygon. A local steward can
commission an existing public crossing without supplying its materials or workers.

## Scope and provenance

Baseline `15ac26b`; registration `9c3937f`; institution/tests `0f0f6d5`;
research harness and experiment source **`a4b42a8`**.
Seven deterministic controlled-delegation runs plus **180 autonomous project runs**:
three configurations × commissioned/pending × seeds 0–29, each 72h.

Appointments, deployment commands in the mechanism controls, and initial commissions
are explicit experimental inputs. They are NOT an autonomous appointment, travel-to-office,
replacement or government-planning policy. In project runs all 50 people—including
sovereign and steward—use the unchanged autonomous policy after the initial commands.
Nobody is required to reside, donate or build. No office is preseeded: each is issued
through the real local appointment action.

The project opportunities deliberately start everyone at the worksite, use an
existing F6 recipe and, except discovery, documented prior memories. They do not
replace F6 or establish that an uninformed kingdom spontaneously coordinates a bridge.
Across all 30 seeds, each project's measured physical outcomes were identical.
These are paired reproducibility/conditional-mechanism results, not 30 distinct
social histories or evidence of statistical robustness to untested worlds.

## Institution implemented

- `Polity(id, sovereign, commons)` contains no beliefs, resources or decision engine.
- `Office(id, polity, holder, settlement, appointment)` records a stewardship separately
  from its holder. Sovereign and candidate must be alive and at the same node.
- The assignment identity is personally knowable from that meeting. No route, resource
  state or remote office intelligence is conveyed. Appointment does not alter the
  candidate's action, goal, route memory or inventory.
- Effective administration requires a valid appointment, a living holder and physical
  presence at the assigned settlement. Queuing, resting or working there does not
  erase presence; beginning an edge traversal does. No residence obligation exists.
- Sovereign death prevents new appointments; existing delegations and the Crown's
  identity persist. Holder death leaves a historical office but removes effective
  administration. No automatic replacement, succession or inheritance.
- Local observation exposes present holders and locally posted commissions. Server/
  research queries derive the true administered set; they are not NPC inputs or a
  proposed omniscient player map.
- In authority-enabled configurations, existing public crossing opportunities require
  a local commission before donation/building. Only the assigned, present steward
  can issue it. Completed infrastructure remains `Commons(0)`, not personal/Crown property.
- A lawful commission remains posted after the issuing steward leaves or dies. Current
  administration and a previously authorized objective are deliberately different.
  No remote wakeup, cancellation or memory refresh is introduced.

Multiple appointments are allowed; repeated identical appointments are rejected.
A holder can have more than one assignment but can only be physically present in
one location. Active hours are counted as settlement unions, not doubled when two
holders are present. No exclusive borders or competing-polity enforcement is modeled.

## Geography and embodiment

These controls use 50 existing people, C—X—F, 12h nutrition, 4h fatigue and 3000
original-age food quanta at F. No autonomous feeding or sleeping commands are issued
in this stratum. It is deliberately a deployment-without-provisioning control,
not an estimate of how long a properly provisioned administration lasts.
All people die at 36h; all food remains physical and spoils at 48h.


No replacement attempt is made; death cannot create another official or move one.

## Authority enabling existing voluntary construction

All project runs start 50 agents at X. Agent 0 physically appoints agent 1 there for
X in both arms. Only the commissioned arm additionally posts the local objective.
C—X remains 0.25h/cap4; X—F starts closed, then becomes 0.25h/cap4 only upon physical
completion. The recipe is **20 wood + 8h nominal work**. Food remains X300/F2700,
created at 0 and expiring at 48h. No new farming, capital, roads or information channel.

Per-run outcomes at 72h (identical for every seed):

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Sufficient, pending | 0 | 0 | 0 / 0 | 300 | 0 | 1200 |
| Sufficient, commissioned | 30 | 20 | 20 / 3 | 1991 | 40 | 726.017 |
| Scarce, pending | 0 | 0 | 0 / 0 | 300 | 0 | 1200 |
| Scarce, commissioned | 0 | 5 | 5 / 0 | 300 | 0 | 1200 |
| Discovery, pending | 0 | 0 | 0 / 0 | 300 | 0 | 1200 |
| Discovery, commissioned | 0 | 0 | 0 / 0 | 300 | 0 | 1200 |

The sufficient crossing completes at **8.055556h**, with **8.005556 actual labor-hours**
from three voluntary workers. Existing timing/impairment/interruptions are preserved;
nominal recipe work is not asserted to equal all elapsed labor. Twenty donors each
contribute a real personally gathered wood unit. Thirty is the number of replicated
completed projects; one crossing exists per run.

The completed route has **38 distinct users and 400 completed improved trips**.
Communal food delivered is 58 quanta. Total queue time grows from **22.406 to 815.055
person-hours**, so authority and construction do not remove congestion. Food wasted
at expiry falls from 2700 to 1009 quanta. Forty alive at 72h does not mean adequate
nourishment: substantial starvation remains, and finite food is exhausted/spoiled.
These are conditional access benefits, not a general claim that authority improves welfare.

Causal trace, sufficient commissioned seed 0:

```
0h          local appointment and local commission; no materials/work created
0h          agents choose wood gathering using existing conditional access valuation
0.000556h   20 real one-unit donations fully supply the recipe
0.016667h   sovereign agent 0 voluntarily starts construction as an ordinary person
0.050000h   steward leaves for remembered food-board work; local authority ceases
1.300000h   steward returns; local authority resumes
5.033333h   steward later works on the same public project
6.033333h   agent 5 takes over construction
8.055556h   project physically completes, owned by Commons(0)
afterward   independent route choices use the new edge and compete for its capacity
```

The commission does not appoint builders. Same-time competing work attempts can be
rejected by the pre-existing exclusive worker rule. The identity that issues an
objective is not guaranteed to execute or finish it.

Scarce case: five legal donations, no construction work because 15 wood are missing.
The command neither creates those materials nor authorizes seizure. Discovery case:
the crossing and local wood are observable, but food behind the closed edge is not.
The unchanged investment policy has no known access benefit; no wood is donated.
The steward leaves at 1.033333h to explore another unknown node. A royal/public
objective does not supply hidden knowledge or manufacture motivation.

Across the project matrix: **180 physical appointments, 90 commissions, 30 completed
crossings**. The remaining 60 commissioned projects are incomplete at the horizon,
not secretly canceled: 30 lack physical materials and 30 attract no participation.
The 90 uncommissioned controls have no authorized objective.

## Administrative presence and real costs

| Project condition | Active h | Away/dead h | Departures | Steward food | Steward travel / queue h | Holder death |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Sufficient pending | 34.500 | 37.500 | 1 | 0 | 0.500 / 1.000 | 36h |
| Sufficient commissioned | 42.831 | 29.169 | 14 | 52 | 10.125 / 15.358 | None by 72h |
| Scarce commissioned | 35.750 | 36.250 | 1 | 1 | 0.500 / 0.733 | 37h |
| Discovery, either arm | 1.033 | 70.967 | 1 | 6 | 0.250 / 0 | 42h |

Scarce pending equals sufficient pending. In the successful arm the sovereign also
consumes 47 quanta, travels/works under ordinary rules and has zero nutrition at 72h;
the steward has zero nutrition too. Institutional identity grants no physiological
protection. All times are directly integrated on the authoritative clock and active
durations independently reconstruct from arrival/departure/death transitions.

These are **gross embodied costs**, not incremental administrative overhead. Holding
an office is not a separate labor action in this minimal institution. Presence may
coincide with sleep, queueing, food acquisition or construction; those hours must
not be counted as a second exclusive activity. The research does not value
opportunity costs in money or presume how much civilian work an official displaced.

## Validation and gate

**183 tests pass**, including ten new authority tests. Clippy across all targets
with warnings denied and formatting pass. Research journal state is excluded from
simulation hashes; removing it does not change actions or events.

**40 historical regression runs match exactly**, including checkpoint physical and
state/event hashes: 19 original provisioning scenarios, 14 F9 direct/market runs
(including low-cost seed18), four F6 crossing cases and three F7 path/capital cases.
All sampled seed0 event traces match byte-for-byte. The only config-print addition
is the optional `authority: None` field; historical output files are not rewritten.
All **3,131 previously existing research files** remain byte-identical.
Every new run has zero material-accounting errors and zero FIFO overtakes.

The gate passes because:

1. Sovereign 0, polity17 and office IDs are distinct records; people still die.
2. Typed appointment requires real colocation and does not transport the holder.
3. Arrival/departure/death changes actual local administration, not historical title.
4. Distance and congestion change establishment time and can prevent it entirely.
5. Dead holders remove authority with no replacement shortcut.
6. Territory derives from active offices; two actual people can cover two settlements.
7. Lawful local commissions enable existing voluntary physical construction; both
   material and information failures survive unchanged.
8. No global office knowledge is passed to policy; no remote orders, resource grants,
   forced labor, money, taxes, coercion or military systems were introduced.

This does not validate legitimacy emergence, autonomous political recruitment,
continuous residence, replacement, communication, enforcement, taxation, or efficient
administration. Those are outside F10. F11 has not been implemented. Evidence comes
before any new political or military power.
