# F11A — voluntary mobilization evidence

Voluntary mobilization works at mechanism scope. The full F11A gate is **not marked PASSED**: protective benefit remains unverified, so F11B formation/movement and F11C combat have not been implemented. No conscription, requisition, taxes, military wages, new supplies, special movement layer or UI were added.

## Implementation and interpretation limits

Source `4422718` (core `b081523`, service-expiry correction `a898be6`). Soldiers are existing Persons with a separate, temporary role indexed by muster/unit ID. Their biological identity, household, memories, relationships, property and physiology remain. A local notice is remembered; a voluntary undertaking travels through existing FIFO; only physical admission activates the role. Notices grant no resource/access rights. The fixed two-hour term expires even if interrupted. Eating/sleep can pause duty; an overriding goal can withdraw without legal penalty. Death uses ordinary Person death and preserves property ownership.

The explicit new behavioral assumption is willingness to consider a known security request before ordinary civilian work when existing survival/care/reproductive priorities permit, no communal job is held, and remembered-route travel plus service passes the six-hour nutrition margin,22h fatigue threshold and known expiry. This is an inspectable provisional civic motive, not established human psychology or patriotism. One attempt per person/request is disclosed. It is not an unconditional demand-to-enlist rule. Request targets constrain local attendance rather than assign workers; a remote volunteer does not reserve a place. Requests are seeded institutional initial conditions, not autonomous recruitment by an omniscient ruler.

### What the traces establish

- **Local admission is not worker assignment.** Four people volunteer for the small request, but only one joins. Three make an unnecessary journey and learn locally that admission is closed. The large request produces four arrivals and eight duty-hours; it does not create the additional four people requested.
- **Geography costs real time.** Four volunteers spend4 travel-hours in the ordinary one-hour route and24 in the six-hour route. The capacity-one fixture adds6 muster-queue hours, and12.069 total queue hours including later civilian traffic. No overtakes occur.
- **Other people can prevent a muster.** In the civilian-traffic case, nine people eventually volunteer after locally learning the request; none traverse toward the muster while committed. They spend22.067 person-hours queued and withdraw when the remembered-route/time/survival check no longer passes. Total queue time rises by22.067h; starvation remains90.120h in both arms. No priority lane or forced persistence was introduced.
- **The timing of service changes the civilian hole it leaves.** Posting before cultivation starts removes13.533 farming-hours, reduces food production96→0 and consumption104→24. Nobody has a personal crop to motivate returning from the empty muster node. Posting during real sowing interrupts all four farmers, but their personally owned unfinished crops persist. After service they independently return toward remembered crops, finish the existing work and produce96 quanta. Farming time increases13.533→14h; consumption increases104→116, with final nutrition20→23h/person. Neither case changes24h starvation. More consumption is an observed timing/allocation effect, not proof of security benefit or long-run welfare.
- **Care still competes before service.** The caregiver feeds first, then volunteers one decision-minute later. Kinship and the directed care link remain. Total explicit care transfers rise13→17 quanta in the enabled run; household separation falls17.967→4h because the altered movement sequence returns the caregiver sooner than the civilian exploration control. The dependent still experiences12 starvation-hours in both arms. More transferred food does not establish adequate care. Instantaneous transfers do not acquire invented labor-hours.
- **Social history remains relevant without military loyalty parameters.** Both B2 histories eventually produce one volunteer/arrival. Costly-help history first causes a6-quantum care gift and delays enlistment one minute; refusal history does not give that gift. The history changes a competing choice, not the eventual enlistment count. No difference was forced.
- **Ignorance and caution still block recruitment.** Unknown-request and food-constrained arms produce zero volunteers. Children are explicitly ineligible. Gate counts are repeated evaluations, not independent people; `counts.csv` reports unique people separately and reasons can overlap across time.

## Resource and household accounting

All duty is embodied awake time. Soldiers' biological reserves diminish using ordinary physiology. In these short autonomous terms, **zero food quanta are consumed during the active Soldier role**; eating happens outside that interval. The dedicated service-eating test verifies real stock decrement and ordinary access/ceiling rules, but the live panel does not demonstrate an army supply chain. No carts appear in this panel: the construction/use test separately proves an owner-built cart travels with its volunteering owner and service grants no right to another owner's cart.

Construction and directed care-travel hours are zero in these fixtures. Therefore no lost construction or care-travel hours are established. Reported travel differences include exploration/migration as well as resource transport and must not be relabeled lost hauling hours. `activity.csv` and `people.csv` retain the distinctions. `timeline.csv` records dependents lacking a living, colocated, non-serving caregiver; this is physical support availability, not guaranteed feeding. All enabled-minus-disabled starvation and death differences are zero over this24h panel; several absolute starvation totals are nonzero. Do not infer survival after the horizon or military nourishment from those zeros.

## Gate disposition

F11A1–8 and10 have bounded mechanism evidence: voluntary choice, physical joining, persistent Persons, excluded civilian work, household consequences, real nutrition/goods, shared transport and continuing B0–B3 behavior. Gate9 is **unverified at protective-benefit scope**. The code represents an explicitly requested protective undertaking but has no threat/protection mechanics with which to measure prevented harm. Changes in food consumption or completing a posted attendance request do not prove protection. This is a scope/evidence gap, not a reason to tune enlistment or fabricate an economic/security bonus.

F11A remains BLOCKED at the full gate, with its mobilization mechanism validated. F11B and F11C remain NOT STARTED. Deciding to carry protective-benefit validation into a later combat/security gate would revise the current gate; it has not been assumed. No compulsory-service institution is authorized or implemented.
