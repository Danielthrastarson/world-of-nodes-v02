# F6 communal crossing rights — accepted

Owner decision: completed crossing.owner = Commons(0). Builder, owner and user
are separate. Community members may use it under actual location, duration,
shared capacity and FIFO constraints. All F6 agents belong to that community;
outsiders, border/customs access, tolls, rental and enforcement are deferred.
No builder land title or exclusive crossing right is inferred.

Minimal input institution: voluntary local donation of personally owned wood to
an identified communal construction project explicitly transfers those inputs to
Commons(0). Donations cannot be remotely made, taken from another owner, exceed
the project's remaining recipe, or be reclaimed automatically. A donor dying does
not reclaim donated public inputs. Personal wood not donated keeps its owner on
death. Anyone in the community may provide local construction labor; no assignments,
wages or automatic worker quotas. One physical work position per crossing is an
explicit initial construction-capacity abstraction. Completion records all donors
and laborers and the finishing builder independently from communal ownership.

See [registered experiment](INFRASTRUCTURE_EXPERIMENT.md) and the documentation-only
[player action language](PLAYER_ACTION_LANGUAGE.md). This supersedes the earlier
public-use versus exclusive-use proposal; F5 personal carts remain unchanged.
