# F6 fixed infrastructure — decision prepared, not implemented

F5 establishes movable personal capital, not ownership of land, roads or crossings.
The roadmap requires an owner decision for new property/cooperation institutions.
This is the next genuine fork; cart rights do not authorize either public or
exclusive use of fixed improvements.

## Smallest proposed causal object

Use a constructed crossing/deck on an existing rough edge. No new route, house
benefit, spoilage reduction, global planner or abstract economic bonus. At one
endpoint, an identifiable investor delivers personally owned wood to a persistent
project, performs interruptible work, then creates a fixed traversable improvement.
Incomplete inputs and completed embedded materials remain accounted for. Dead
investor inputs retain their ownership; no inheritance or seizure.

Provisional physical experiment parameters: 20 wood and 8 nominal labor-hours;
completed crossing reduces that edge's nominal traversal duration by 25%, retaining
its capacity, shared FIFO, physiology penalties and one clock. These are new F6
fixture parameters, not edits to any earlier experiment. No wear/repair, automatic
traffic-based construction or F7 path emergence yet. The explicit physical
abstraction is that a deck removes part of the rough crossing delay; it is not a
bonus to food or production. Validate before treating it as a general road model.

An agent may invest only using locally observed edge conditions, remembered demand,
expected own travel and local material availability. No benefit from unknown
travelers is credited. No quotas. Compare enabled/disabled crossing investment
with identical geography and stocks, across a line, branching demand and alternate
routes, and low/high repeated use. Preserve noninvestment and failed payback.

## Choice needed before implementation

A. Public use of the completed improvement. Anyone physically arriving may traverse
it; the investor has no power to exclude travelers. Record the builder without
creating a land title. Investment can yield benefits for others without a promise
of altruism, reimbursement or communal ownership of every surrounding resource.

B. Builder-exclusive use of the improvement. The builder alone may receive its
traversal benefit; others retain the original crossing capability. This adds an
explicit access institution and would need a physically coherent representation
of improved versus unimproved passage. It must not create free extra FIFO capacity.

Neither is inferred from F5. A is the smaller physical model and my proposed F6
starting institution; B tests exclusion but adds more access semantics. No money,
tolls, sharing agreements, coercion or enforcement system is proposed.

## Gate and reporting

Before expansion: construction consumes delivered inputs, time and labor; location
persists; shared queue correctness and conservation pass; autonomous site selection
responds to remembered travel demand in multiple topologies using the same code.
Report who invests, who benefits, investment loss, route changes, starvation and
ownership/access effects. F6 does not pass merely because a scripted crossing
can be spawned. F7 remains separate until F6 is established.
