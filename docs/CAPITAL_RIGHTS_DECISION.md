# F5 reviewable proposal — cart rights unresolved

F3 and F4 gates pass. No cart code is implemented. The owner explicitly selected
personal crops to isolate renewable production; that decision does not itself
choose access to shared/durable capital. The roadmap reserves choices materially
determining property/cooperation for the owner. This is the next such decision.

## Proposed narrow physical contract (ready for implementation after rights choice)

One handcart, no draft animals, roads or tools. A provisional registered recipe:
10 physically gathered wood quanta +4 nominal labor-hours → cart containing those
10 wood quanta. Work/local materials/time are real and interruptible. No free carts.
Recipe inputs are tracked into the finished object, not silently deleted. Natural
wood remains open-access until gathered under existing personal ownership rules.

Cart has a stable identity, owner, physical location and exclusive user/custodian.
It is hauled by a real agent and shares that agent's travel progress; it cannot
teleport, duplicate loads or occupy two places. Baseline backpack stays10 quanta;
cart holds an additional20 only while physically available. Queues and physiology
are unchanged. A stranded/dead carrier leaves cart/cargo at its actual location.
No durability, repairs, vehicle market, lending contract or technology tree yet.
A parked cart cannot grant a remote capacity bonus. Autonomous agents receive only
local observations/memories of wood, carts and transport opportunities.

The provisional capacity/cost numbers are experimental parameters, not calibrated
for benefit. Register contexts with short and long hauling demand; compare no cart,
physically possible investment schedule and voluntary investment. Measure lost
labor/nourishment now, later loads, total food movement, deaths, waste, underused
capital and opportunity cost. Investment may be harmful. No success guarantee.

## Owner-level fork

A. **Personal capital (recommended for isolation).** The builder contributes its
personally owned inputs and owns the work in progress and finished cart. Owner use
only; another agent cannot seize/use it. Death retains ownership and can strand
capital. No inheritance, lending or communal reassignment. Any later sharing or
transfer institution is a separate decision. This isolates individual investment.

B. **Explicit communal capital.** Inputs must be voluntarily transferred to the
commons; work in progress/cart is Commons-owned. Group use requires a new explicit
local exclusive-access rule. This couples capital investment to cooperation and
allocation. It should be documented as a new institution, not hidden in a utility
bonus or inferred from the existing food ration rule.

Choosing A or B is not a test-tuning decision. It determines whose future benefit
can justify present labor. The physical recipe and research controls above can be
implemented once that institutional assumption is settled. No farming result or
historical policy is changed by this pending decision.
