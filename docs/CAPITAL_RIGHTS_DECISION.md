# F5 capital-rights decision — accepted

The owner selected PERSONAL CAPITAL. Finished cart ownership begins on completion:
`Cart.owner = Agent(builder)`. Before completion, inputs remain personal materials
in an exclusive construction process, not an owned finished cart. Owner and user
are separate. Only the owner may use it; death retains ownership and physical state.
No transfer, inheritance, rental, sharing, money, depreciation or land/road title.

The current owner instruction supersedes the earlier proposal's suggestion that
cart ownership would begin during construction. [Registered F5 experiment](CAPITAL_EXPERIMENT.md).
