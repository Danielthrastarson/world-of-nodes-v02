# World of Nodes foundation roadmap

Owner-authorized 2026-09-22. This roadmap supersedes previous scope prohibitions
only as each dependency gate passes. The locked finite-food reference and every
published result remain preserved. A negative provisioning result is not a failed
software gate. No milestone requires efficiency or universal survival.

Implement → test → explain → pass gate → continue. On failure, stop expansion,
isolate the cause, run a bounded experiment, fix proven defects, and retest.
Technical coherence needs no additional approval. Property rights, cooperation,
monetary/political institutions, player powers, multiplayer fairness, core fantasy,
and irreversible abstraction choices require a project-owner decision.

| F7 Emergent infrastructure | PASSED | Completed civilian travel creates paths; continued use maintains them and disuse reverses wear. Effective travel time changes route/traffic outcomes under unchanged capacity and FIFO. Engineered roads and staffed maintenance remain deferred. |

| F9B Physical local market | PASSED | 360 final paired runs: 30 delayed swaps in controlled co-location; zero swaps and one offer across 120 larger market worlds. Deposit/revocation churn changes allocation without trade. Physical escrow, ownership, locality and controls validated; money remains unauthorized. |

| F10 Territory / authority | PASSED | Physical sovereign/steward appointments, presence-derived administration and local public commissions. Seven mechanism controls + 180 paired runs expose distance, congestion, death and absence; no coercive powers, forced residence or autonomous government planner. |

| F13 Kingdom vertical slice | NOT STARTED | Multiple settlements, economy, infrastructure, authority, information and security; one ruler decision yields unscripted, traceable second-order effects. Owner decides player powers. |

| F15 Rendering | NOT STARTED | World → observation → render snapshot → screen. Real identities/goods/roads/fields/units. Renderer never owns world state or time. |
| F16 Physical visual depth | NOT STARTED | Lighting, shadows, particles, fire, weather, limited physics where causally justified, with LOD. Low geometric complexity, high systemic complexity. |
| F17 Multiplayer foundation | NOT STARTED | Server-compatible kernel, stable IDs, explicit commands, reproducible compact events; small-player correctness before 50–100-player scale. No client-authoritative resources. |

Dependencies follow the listed foundation order. Geography testing accompanies
later systems, not just F1. Geography changes possibility, never causal law.
Research optimizers may inspect global truth; production policy may not. Production
physics, bounded policy, explicit institutions, and research instruments remain
separate. Borrow small established planning/task-allocation/queueing/flow ideas only
when evidence justifies them; no framework adoption by name.

Each completed milestone must link specification, parameters, tests, positive and
negative evidence, causal interpretation, revision and reproduction commands.
Keep old outputs immutable and new studies in distinct directories. The simulation
is authoritative: no silent creation of matter, knowledge, time, rights, labor,
transport, cooperation or productive capacity.

F7 passed at minimal path scope: [420-run evidence](../research/paths/REPORT.md),

F8 passed: [210-run evidence](../research/settlements/REPORT.md),
[registered two-settlement coordination study](TWO_SETTLEMENT_EXPERIMENT.md).
Scarcity, productive capability and allocation remain distinct. Existing Commons(0)
spans two inhabited locations; crop/cart rights and policy remain unchanged. The
research observer measures flows, exclusive labor time, migration, capital, local
need and churn. Barter, money and prices are not presumed solutions.
Short routes permit communal deposits in 6/30 seeds, while migration dominates and
wood does not move between settlements. No cart completes. Paths reduce waiting
but worsen mean nourishment here. Crop claims remain inaccessible after owner death.
These are explained interactions, not efficient trade or sustainability. F9 remains
unstarted: money has not been shown necessary or sufficient. A new barter, pooling,
lending or abandoned-property institution needs an explicit owner decision first.

F9A owner decision resolves **barter only**: [registered experiment](BARTER_EXPERIMENT.md).
Personal goods may be alienated voluntarily. Immediate local atomic barter has no
debt, currency, inheritance, lending or forced sale. Crop, land and cart-title
institutions are not extended. The F8 communal arrival-without-deposit trace is
preserved; trade rights do not repair delivery arbitration. Existing historical
configurations keep barter disabled for reproducibility; F9A enabled arms select
the new institution explicitly. No monetary implementation is authorized.

F9A evidence: [360-run report](../research/barter/REPORT.md). Across enabled F8 worlds
only one offer forms; the meeting fixtures create offers but owners leave before
settlement. No qualifying double-coincidence case occurs in live runs. A separate
static case validates that classifier and another demonstrates useful voluntary
exchange through the real kernel. No resale motive, meeting commitment or trade
search is added afterward. F9B assessment finds no evidence sufficient to choose
money; monetary experiments remain unstarted and require an owner decision.


F9B passed at bounded scope: [registered institution](MARKET_EXPERIMENT.md),
