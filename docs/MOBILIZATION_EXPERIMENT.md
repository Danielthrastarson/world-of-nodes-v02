# F11A preregistration — voluntary muster

## Scope and disclosed assumptions

Requests are typed, explicitly seeded local institutional initial conditions: posting node, muster node, requested attendance, open/expiry times, service duration and `ProtectCrossing` or `DefendSettlement` objective. These are stated objectives, not evidence of an enemy. There is no security bonus, hazard invented to reward attendance, or combat. Target attendance limits local admission; it never assigns workers. Remote volunteers do not reserve seats or update the board.

The new willingness rule is deliberately inspectable: after existing eating, critical survival, sleep, atomic gathering, care and reproduction arbitration, a person with no communal job can consider a known open request. It requires a remembered route, predicted completion before expiry, at least the existing six nutrition-hour margin after travel plus service, and predicted fatigue below the existing22h critical threshold. This is a provisional civic participation assumption, not patriotism, an optimal welfare score, or a proven human model. It makes a safe known service opportunity eligible before ordinary civilian work. Refusals state a gate; demand alone never ensures acceptance.

A volunteer remembers its undertaking; arrival is separately validated locally against capacity and expiry. The same routes/queues apply. Existing priority actions can interrupt; eating/sleep pause duty, while care, civilian work or survival redirection withdraws the undertaking without penalty. Completion is two hours after admission, a fixed announced wall-clock term, not forced labor; active duty time is measured separately from sleep/eating. One attempt per request per person prevents unmeasured reenlistment loops. Death ends the role through ordinary death. Request lifetime12h. Unknown requests cannot motivate a decision. No new information channel. Birth extends personal military memory but adds no knowledge or role.

## Comparisons, before outcomes

Thirty paired seeds0–29,24h duration, disabled/enabled request visibility with otherwise identical physical worlds. Cases: small(local request1), large(request8), food constrained, caregiver, farmer, distant(6h vs1h edge), bottleneck(capacity1 vs4), unknown(remote posting), helpful/refusal social history, and civilian-traffic crossing. Same baseline four adults unless stated; caregiver adds a real child. Matching initial resource/physiology/history per pair. Fixed local initial conditions are not autonomous political recruitment. Do not tune after results.

Record request observations/evaluations, explicit gates, unique volunteers/refusals/arrivals, former activity, travel/queue/duty/active service, food consumption, civilian/soldier starvation, person/household activity and starvation, production, care, carts, migrations/arrivals, shared queues, deaths, exact state/event hashes and selected traces. Compute paired labor differences, including increases; instantaneous gifts have no invented labor cost. Distinguish absence of a living colocated caregiver from actual no feeding, and duty time from the counterfactual labor displaced. Preserved B2 appraisal history may influence participation through existing care arbitration; no new sex or military relationship coefficients.

## Validation and gate discipline

Tests cover locality, refusal, physical mustering, child restriction, role identity, physiology, no property grants, FIFO, withdrawal/completion/death, care interruption and births. Replay historical scenarios with military absent; preserve prior artifacts by SHA256. Parameter/debug schema text may change, authoritative event/state hashes must not.

Mechanism validity does not establish protective benefit. GateA9 (mobilization beneficial or harmful) requires evidence: labor/household costs can be demonstrated here, but no threat-response benefit can be inferred from an attendance count. If a protective benefit cannot be tested without expanding scope, report that gate limitation and stop F11B/C expansion. Do not invent combat or an arbitrary benefit to pass it.
