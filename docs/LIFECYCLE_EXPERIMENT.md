# B3 registration — embodied generations

Baseline fdbc037, B2 accepted. No new inheritance, marriage, mating exclusivity, psychology by sex, household pooling or military. Historical configurations retain lifecycle absent. Biological records are separate embodied Person state with stable IDs; optional life view enters the common Person context.

## Provisional accelerated mechanics

One authoritative millisecond clock. Childhood lasts24h, gestation8h, a mutual reproductive undertaking lasts1h, attempts have32h cooldown for both parties, fertility is available from age24h up to96h. These are accelerated research intervals, not historical-human calibration. No senescence mortality, lactation, miscarriage stochasticity, pregnancy movement/metabolic penalty or genetics. All existing metabolism/starvation/sleep laws apply unchanged to children. A newborn has0 nutrition,0 fatigue and no goods; food is never created by birth. Birth sex is an independent seeded50/50 draw. Conception probability is25%;100%/0% are explicitly labeled mechanical controls.

Only living adult opposite-role partners, physically at the same node, may attempt reproduction. Existing Partner links do not imply marriage or exclusivity. Each participant independently selects the same partner; only matching consents at the same decision time begin a joint undertaking. A unilateral selection never creates pregnancy. Any other physical action cancels an ongoing undertaking; survival and care retain priority. An uninterrupted undertaking consumes elapsed awake time, excluding other labor. The policy considers starting it when idle, no job, nutrition>=12h, fatigue<16h, cooldown elapsed and a locally observed eligible Partner exists. No mate search, remote readiness information, guaranteed partner acceptance or population target. Identical motivation across sexes; an explicit drive-disabled control tests declining consent.

Pregnancy is carried by the gestating Person. Death ends it without birth. Delivery is due after8h; if due during atomic edge travel it waits for the next legal node. This narrow delivery convention is explicit: it does not teleport a baby, consume an extra edge slot or invent infant carrying. Report any due-to-delivery delay. Maternal death takes precedence at coincident boundaries. Birth at the node creates a new persistent Person and extends existing per-person memory stores. Child policy can eat accessible legal food, sleep and wait; it performs no adult labor. Physical travel remains possible as an explicit command, without carrying/teleportation. Kernel rejects child economic, office and reproductive actions, not merely policy.

At birth, both parentage relations and both structural caregiver obligations are created. Child joins the gestating parent's household; if absent, create a household for that parent and child only. Separate households do not merge. No property transfers at birth. New parent-child social dimensions start0 and are recorded as personally encountered only on local observation; no remote emotional update. Structural kinship is not a hunger/death report. Existing B2 selection coefficients remain unchanged, including possible failure to care with zero initial attachment. No substitute caregiver appears on death. Maturation changes capabilities at the exact age boundary without replacing identity, belongings or history.
