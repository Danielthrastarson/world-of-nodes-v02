# F13: owner decision on the first ruler interaction

**Historical proposal, superseded by the owner's F13B scoped office-obligation decision.**
See [the adopted narrow contract](COMMAND_AUTHORITY.md). The options below remain
for provenance; none was silently adopted.

Status: proposal only. No option below is adopted by this document. F12 establishes transported evidence; it does not establish an entitlement to another person's labor or obedience.

F13A can continue with research-controlled **local steward commissioning** under the existing institution. It can test whether a lawful commission propagates through construction, movement, production, households and security. That requires no new remote command right. Label the issuing action and any prepared appointments as controlled inputs. A successful readiness experiment does not establish autonomous political initiative or a player-to-steward command channel.

## Existing authority and the missing boundary

| Existing mechanism | What it authorizes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| `AppointSteward`, validated in [authority.rs](../src/provisioning/authority.rs) | A living adult sovereign may appoint a living adult candidate physically colocated at a node, for a registered settlement. | Appointment supplies neither a route nor a travel goal. There is no automatic obedience, courier service or remote appointment. The current mechanism does not model a separate candidate-consent exchange. |
| `CommissionCrossing`, validated in [authority.rs](../src/provisioning/authority.rs) | The holder of an active local steward office may authorize an available project at that office's settlement. | Crown identity alone does not confer this operation. It requires the local office holder. No materials, labor, resource seizure or guaranteed completion follow from authorization. |
| Local authority and territory | A valid office with a living, physically present holder provides active administration; territory is derived from that presence. | An appointment record does not reveal current remote presence to the ruler. The true footprint is a research/kernel query, not player knowledge. |
| `issue_unit_move`, in [formation/adapter.rs](../src/provisioning/formation/adapter.rs) | A research/local mechanism delivers a movement objective to assembled members physically present at the supplied issue node. | Its signature has no issuer or office check. It must not be exposed as unrestricted royal command authority. Voluntary service has not specified who may redirect the unit, permissible objectives or how consent covers subsequent orders. |
| F12 reports and courier undertakings | A person can communicate a held, dated local observation through actual contact. A controlled delivery purpose can use ordinary travel. | Reports are not orders or permissions. Assigned research courier purposes do not prove voluntary recruitment, official courier duties or a ruler's right to dispatch a person. |

The owner decision concerns **who the player acts through, what may be addressed to another person, and what obligation—if any—receipt creates**. Communication, authority and willingness remain separate checks.

## Three bounded options

### A. Local Crown actions only

The player acts through the embodied sovereign. The first interaction exposes personal observation/report inspection and the existing physically colocated appointment operation. It does not give the player direct control of every steward.

This adds the least institutional scope. Its limitation is deliberate: appointment alone may cause no travel, project or economic change under the present policy. Do not fill that gap by silently assigning an office holder a mandatory undertaking. Local steward commissioning can remain an independent controlled F13A input until delegated initiative is explicitly designed.

Bounded study if selected: pair a lawful appointment with no appointment under the same physical state; vary local presence, subsequent departure and death. Compare actual administration with the sovereign's known administration. Record zero downstream project activity as a valid outcome.

### B. Advisory civil project requests — recommended

The embodied sovereign may **request** one existing crossing project from an appointed steward. Receipt supplies an objective for consideration, not an instruction that automatically executes. The steward may decline, defer or, when physically present with the existing legal authority, commission the project. Workers and owners retain their present choices and property rights.

Keep the first version to one project type, one addressed recipient and one request. A report may justify the request, but must not itself become an executable command. Any courier participation also needs an explicit voluntary acceptance rule; no official labor obligation is inferred. The owner should approve these advisory semantics before implementation. Technical representation and instrumentation can then proceed without repeated approval.

This is the recommended first complete ruler-to-world cascade because it reuses the already authorized local commissioning operation and leaves coercive powers absent. It still introduces new behavioral hypotheses: considering a sovereign's request and volunteering to carry it. Register those hypotheses rather than presenting them as mechanical bug fixes.

Bounded study if selected: first isolate local receipt versus no request with identical personal information; then compare the same typed request delivered physically over short, long and congested routes. Keep transport and request-acceptance effects separate. Measure delivery age, refusal/deferral, office presence at attempted commission, material/labor participation, construction, changed civilian movement, care, nourishment and any security effect. A request that never produces a commission is informative.

### C. Limited commands within voluntary military service

The owner designates which embodied issuer or office may command an existing unit and what scope members accepted when volunteering. The initial scope could be Move/Hold/Withdraw during the existing term; it must not silently extend service, permit new recruitment or authorize private-resource use. Combat objectives require an explicit scope decision rather than being inferred from Move.

Orders reach members locally or through a separately validated physical command-delivery mechanism. No absent member reads the unit's global objective. Survival interruptions, service expiry and ordinary Person state remain effective.

Bounded study if selected: identical voluntary unit and threat, varied authorized issuer presence and order delay; compare against the existing research-injected order control. Record rejected unauthorized orders, actual receipt, stragglers, expiry, protection and civilian cost. This is a larger institutional step than advisory civil requests and is not required to finish local F13A readiness research.

## Knowledge and interface constraints for every option

Keep the next increment headless. A later knowledge viewer must show the ruler's own observations and received reports, including origin, observation time, receipt time and whether a newer direct observation superseded the report. Display unknown as unknown. Do not fill gaps from true stocks, unit rosters, remote bodies, true office activity or research journals.

Command validation may reject an attempted operation using authoritative local mechanics; it must not disclose an otherwise unobserved remote fact as an error message. Report receipt and request receipt must remain distinguishable. Requests, acceptance, attempted execution and completion need separate records; an issued objective is not evidence of completion.

All options preserve the boundaries already reserved for owner decisions: no conscription, requisition, taxation, inheritance, land title, seizure, currency, criminal punishment, conquest-to-sovereignty rule or population aggregation. No new rights are adopted to rescue a failed outcome.

The decision does **not** gate all F13 work. Complete and preserve the local commissioning readiness evidence first. Stop when the next implementation would select one of these player/delegation semantics, rather than treating a research control as an already legitimate player power.
