# F0: mechanical adoption and conditional meals

Registered starting revision: 33dc4b1. Owner authorized adoption of six proven
mechanical corrections, not meal planning or any new commitment preference.
All 700 earlier research files are SHA-256 protected in research/meals.

Normal `Current` now selects the validated coherent no-meal estimator. Explicit
`LegacyForecast` pins historical reproduction entry points; old meal/commitment
experiments remain historical. Forecast reasons/event strings can change while
physical outcomes stay identical. All physical laws and parameters stay unchanged.

Live arms: Current (corrected, no meal) and ConditionalMeals (research only).
Each uses seeds 0–29, 50 at C, 600-quanta request at C, no inherited source
knowledge, 3000 quanta at F created at zero, expiry48h, cap10, 2h edges, shared
capacity4, identical physiology, FIFO, six-hour final margin and priority hierarchy.
72h endpoint; 36/48/72h population checks. No commitment or source-prior intervention.

The only added planning operation is legal consumption of food actually carried
or conditionally acquired in the tape. Original age/ownership survive acquisition;
observed available quantity, request target and carrying capacity bound acquisition.
Eating reduces potential delivery. Unknown future availability is NOT guaranteed.
No query to World is permitted by the forecast API.

Both arms schedule primitive kernel actions identically. Claims, loose pickup and
eating have zero physical duration in the kernel. Neither arm inserts policy-tick
pauses into the conditional tape. This evaluates executable physical possibility,
not a promise that the unchanged reactive policy will choose every subsequent step.
The earlier meal estimator added tick pauses; it is retained for historical replay.
Offline old-meal/new-meal differences therefore include that explicit scheduling
convention as well as the six corrections; do not attribute all to one defect.

Before live runs: full suite, earlier scenario hash reproduction, 540 no-meal and
540 meal static conditional kernel comparisons, adopted Current vs previously tested
CoherentForecast equivalence on all original topology/parameter scenarios.
Observers must preserve state/events/RNG. Per-seed reports separate acceptance
evaluations, individual candidates, conditional physical feasibility, margin PASS,
board-directed decisions, actual queue joins/arrivals and accepted-job chain.
PASS meal records contain food evidence, quantities, nutrition before/after/final,
and deliverable amount. Matched-clock decisions across divergent live worlds are
trajectory comparisons, not same-belief causal comparisons. Offline comparisons
use exactly the same personal inputs/RNG. Seeds may produce identical histories;
report that instead of implying independent stochastic replication.

Do not use survivor counts alone: starvation exposure, consumption, waste,
migration, carrier consumption and delivered quantity are mandatory.
