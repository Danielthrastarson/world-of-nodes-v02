# Delivery decomposition — research increment

The original P1 reference and all `research/provisioning/` evidence are frozen.
Question: is zero delivery physically necessary, or a consequence of decisions,
information and institutions? No physical parameter or production system changes.

Two separate instruments:

* A research-only scheduler submits validated physical intents with autonomous
  decisions disabled. Its globally informed schedules are feasibility witnesses,
  never NPC policies. Relaxed analytical bounds are reported separately from
  schedules actually achieved; a best found schedule is not a proven maximum.
* A delivery fixture posts 600 quanta of outstanding demand at C at t=0 (50 times
  the communal 12h ceiling). This is an explicit board initial condition, retained
  until delivered, not a remote hunger feed. Local shortage refresh can exceed
  this recorded backlog. Agents retain reference initial knowledge; F is unknown.

Compare seeds 0–29 for current, queue commitment, sleep commitment and job
commitment individually. The existing job intention is already persistent;
its explicit cancellation conditions will be tested, not silently replaced.
Claims already expire after 12h without cancelling workers: measure this directly
in accepted-job fixtures rather than inventing a redundant intervention.

Physical support must distinguish continuously nourished C residents from alive
but starving residents. Report support horizons explicitly. Earliest witness,
rigorous time lower bound, attained delivery and rigorous quantity upper bound
are distinct; report unresolved optimality rather than labelling a heuristic
schedule an exact maximum. No combined policy intervention is initially run.
