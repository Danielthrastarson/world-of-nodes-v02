# F6 registered shared crossing experiment

Scope: one physical fixed improvement on an existing edge or explicitly blocked
crossing. 20 wood + 8 nominal work-hours. Completed improvement either reduces
nominal crossing work by 25% with unchanged capacity, or opens the previously
blocked crossing with its documented capacity. All changes operate through the
same movement, impairment, occupancy and FIFO mechanics. No economic bonus.
Materials travel in actual inventories before local donation. One local work
position; work can pause or change worker without loss of progress. No ruler API.

Active traversals retain the movement work requirement recorded at admission;
opening/improving an edge does not teleport or retroactively accelerate somebody
already crossing. New arrivals use current physical infrastructure. Observations
expose only adjacent effective edge properties. Remote memories stay stale.

Policy extension: a bounded personal-memory comparison of known food access and
remembered hauling demand with/without an observed possible improvement. No
unknown food benefit or presumed future travelers is credited. Expected repeated
trip savings must justify the project's remaining construction time, or construction
must open access to remembered edible food unavailable by the remembered graph.
Food must be expected to remain edible after construction and travel. This is a
conditional opportunity heuristic, not global scheduling or a promise of payoff.
Existing critical hunger/sleep override construction. Work is reconsidered in
bounded increments using the existing mechanical work forecast; no all-day
uninterruptible labor. Actual resource contribution is voluntary and local.

Registered 72h paired fixtures (30 seeds, available/unavailable):
- closed: C-X-F, .25h edges, X-F blocked until built; capacity4 after opening;
  300 food at X and 2700 at F; 100 wood C; worksite X.
- short: same map/stocks, X-F already open; reduce .25h to .1875h if built.
- bottleneck: closed fixture but opened crossing capacity1.
- alternate: closed fixture plus X-B-F alternative of two 2h edges; no new route
  appears magically, construction opens the already observed blocked shortcut.
- scarce: closed fixture with only5 wood C.
- discovery: closed fixture without inherited source/site/wood information.
All other fixtures have explicit timestamped t=0 prior observations of graph,
food, wood and construction opportunities, identically in both arms. This is an
information-initialization control only; live policy cannot query remote truth.
All start50 at C, nutrition12/fatigue4, bag10, all3000food created0/expiry48,
initial communal food request600 at C, no farming or carts, unchanged physiology.
Construction availability is the only paired intervention. No parameter tuning
after results. Additional static/matched controls may isolate a proven defect,
not replace unfavorable autonomous outcomes.

Record attempts, contributions/donors, starts/completions, wood/labor, completion
clock time, builders/users, edge traffic and food/wood flow, queue waits/occupancy,
route changes, node arrivals/migration, newly accessible F access, starvation,
consumption/waste/deaths and exact accounting. Preserve full seed-zero event traces,
per-agent endpoints and per-seed metrics. Compare original artifacts/hashes before
adoption. Gate requires physical construction, multiple autonomous users, actual
movement effects across geography, explicit externalities and intact invariants.
Failures halt expansion and trigger diagnosis, not invisible balancing.
