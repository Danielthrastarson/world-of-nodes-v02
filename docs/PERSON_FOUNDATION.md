## Refactor contract

Keep one existing embodied human record and stable IDs. Expose `Person` as its
canonical architectural name while retaining `Agent` source/debug compatibility.
Do not duplicate people, inventories, brains or clocks. Ownership/custody remain
in the goods ledger; crops/carts/offices keep their existing independent identities.

Replace role-like runtime capability branches with one personal decision input:
current local observation, timestamped spatial/production/capital/infrastructure
beliefs and enabled action capabilities. Preserve the exact existing ordering,
thresholds, forecast modes, interruption semantics and random draws. Specialized
consider functions supply domain opportunities; they are not distinct person types.

Extract barter action selection into a pure personal-input evaluator. It receives
only the existing local barter view and locally inspectable escrow backing. Return
observer notes separately; World applies the same deduplication, research-only
classification and event logs in the same order. Omniscient coincidence diagnostics
must never supply a value to the choice. Preserve market record locality and
custody-dependent valuation churn, even where outcomes look undesirable.

Use typed read-only person state for needs, current route goal, accepted undertaking,
atomic action, personal roles and recognized rights. Roles are actual sovereign /
appointed-office identities, not permanent farmer/builder/merchant classes. Rights
summaries cannot bypass authoritative physical/institutional validation. Known
possessions are carried/local observations and timestamped goods memories, never
a global owner's-inventory scan. Holding an office confers no new work preference.

A selected plan is the next proposed atomic command plus its existing undertaking,
route goal and causal reason. Do not pretend the reactive policy has an unimplemented
persistent multi-step plan. Existing candidate forecasts are conditional plans;
accepted jobs remain the existing longer undertaking. No BDI framework, GOAP search,
new optimizer, stronger commitment or goal utility coefficient is added.

Physical execution remains the existing finite action-state machine. Observations
refresh beliefs before selection; observed consequences affect the next decision.
Immediate survival retains its existing priority. Institutional commands use that
same executor. F10 appointment/commission choices remain explicitly commanded:
B0 does not invent a steward/government policy or call it autonomous.

Social relations/affect/life-cycle capabilities remain unmodeled until B1/B2/B3.
Do not represent absence of those systems as zero trust/attachment or fake emotional
state. Document extension boundaries rather than filling a person with unused stats.
