# Post-F11C execution plan

Audit baseline: `2b00dd9`. The current roadmap remains valid; split F12 into dependency gates rather than changing its position. Independent source/evidence reviews confirm that information transport is missing, while the accepted military mechanism does not yet define sovereign command rights. All earlier historical status paragraphs remain preserved.

Dependency graph:

F11C → F12A (bounded report provenance/handoff) → F12B (embodied courier execution) → F12C (delayed-information decision experiments) → F13 command-authority decision → headless kingdom slice → later gameplay/rendering.

F12A uses immutable local observation reports, explicit recipients and physically colocated handoff. Original observation time, author and receipt time remain distinct. Old reports cannot overwrite newer personal observations. No remote acknowledgements or authority changes.

F12B adds an optional report-delivery undertaking to the common Person selector. Assigning the initial undertaking is a disclosed research input, not royal compulsory labor or spontaneous messenger formation. Existing eating, critical survival, recovery, care, reproductive, combat and service commitments retain precedence. Ordinary remembered routes and FIFO execute transport; a missed rendezvous does not reveal the recipient's remote location.

F12C tests information alone with matched physical pre-receipt states, and the full delivery intervention with paired delivered/unassigned worlds. The latter includes both transport effort and information effects and must not be described as a pure information treatment. Report truth, age, receipt latency, decisions, resource use and failed delivery are measured separately. Negative welfare and stale-report results remain valid.

F13 preparation may document typed command boundaries, but the existing research unit-order entry has no issuer/office authority check. Before exposing it as sovereign power, ask who may command voluntarily serving people and what their service consent authorizes. No courier labor entitlement, remote commission power or military compulsion is inferred from report transport. Stop at that real owner boundary after F12's evidence is complete.

Engineering debt is tracked separately: existing per-person world observation and combat scans can be quadratic. Communication adds no all-to-all exchange scan or global dissemination. No claim of population-scale performance follows from small fixtures.

## Dependency refinement after F12 evidence

F12's840-run panel and217 historical replays pass. Proceed through an F13A research-only cross-system readiness control using an already authorized local steward commission. This safe integration does not need new royal command rights. Then stop at the concrete player/authority contract: appointment currently assigns an office but does not command residence, labor or project execution; the unit-order research API likewise has no sovereign issuer check. Full F13 remains open until a selected lawful player action is defined. The original plan above records the initial broader boundary; this refinement avoids treating it as a blanket block on integration work.
