# F5 registered personal-capital study

Fixed recipe:10 wood +4 nominal work-hours → one handcart, with20 cargo quanta in
addition to the unchanged10 backpack. Owner assigned at completion. Construction
retains invested material and progress on interruption/death; no scavenging. Wood
is finite, locally observed, open-access until personally gathered (2s/quantum, same
impairment as existing gathering). Inputs become physical embedded wood, counted
in accounting. Only owner may hitch/unhitch/use a local cart. One person can pull
one cart. A cart shares its user's physical movement and FIFO slot; there is no
terrain/speed bonus. Cargo is physically in that identified container, keeps age
and goods ownership, and remains with a stranded cart after user death.

Investment is a bounded conditional comparison using personal memory only:
remembered source + remembered communal demand; estimate repeated return-trip work
at backpack versus cart capacity, limited by source quantity, demand, expiry horizon
and current believed route costs. Compare saved trip time with wood-gathering and
construction work. Require construction to preserve the existing6h nutrition margin;
no planned meals credited. Existing immediate food/sleep needs override. No job or
source knowledge means no invented hauling demand. A local unfinished investment
may be resumed when the comparison still holds. Existing carts can be used locally;
no automatic sharing or assignment. These are disclosed provisional preferences,
not corrections intended to guarantee payoff. No generic BDI/GOAP adoption.

Paired 30seeds (0–29),72h,50 agents at C,3000food at F created0/expiry48, edgecap4,
backpack10, nutrition12/fatigue4, C-X-F. Registered cases:
- short_low:0.25h edges,30-quanta initial C request,500wood at C
- short_high:0.25h edges,600request,500wood C
- medium_high:0.5h edges,600request,500wood C
- long_high:2h edges,600request,500wood C
- wood_scarce:0.25h edges,600request,5wood C
- wood_contested:0.25h edges,600request,10wood C
- discovery:0.5h edges,600request,500wood C, no inherited knowledge
Other cases explicitly begin with documented remembered route/source information
through the existing prior-memory research initializer, identically in both arms.
Current local C board/wood are physically observed. These are new research fixtures,
not edits to any historical reference. Compare cart unavailable vs available while
wood physically exists in both. No tuning after outcomes.

Tests include local exclusive access; completed ownership; material transformation;
interrupt/death during construction; exact work forecast; parking removes capacity;
mid-edge death; cart cargo spoilage; FIFO/capacity; no hidden remote wood or demand;
no-food/no-wood/no-labor controls; historical hashes; same decisions under identical
personal histories. Research schedules also demonstrate physical carrying effects
and opportunity costs without replacing autonomous policy.

Metrics: considerations/rejections, starts/completions, wood committed, labor, first
use delay, cart trips/food-quanta-edge moved, backpack flow, utilization, starvation,
deaths, idle cart time, dead-owner lock-up. Count edge crossings separately from
unique deliveries. Per-cart excess over backpack capacity on matched traversals is
a capability witness, NOT causal payback. Causal physical payback uses paired world
throughput differences and explicit matched research schedules; retain wood cost,
labor and delay as separate units, never subtract unlike units or invent money.
Wood has no competing non-cart use yet: scarcity tests alternative cart investments,
not a fabricated housing/tool opportunity cost. F5 gate requires actual autonomous
investment plus physically demonstrated costs/benefits; survival improvement optional.
