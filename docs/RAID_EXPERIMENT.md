# F11C registered physical raid/contact experiment

F11B accepted; F11A full protective-benefit gate remains open pending evidence. Owner now selects a raid on physical communal food, superseding the earlier proposed civilian victim in THREAT_EXPERIMENT.md. No combat results exist at registration.

## Minimal laws

Opt-in body state belongs to each Person, not a Unit: 0 injuries functional,1 wounded,2 incapacitated,3 dead. One wound halves existing physiology-based movement/work/strike progress. Incapacitated people cannot move, work, reproduce or fight; they can consume accessible food, rest and receive existing care. No healing or automatic death timer. Three simultaneous injuries can kill; nobody automatically executes incapacitated people. Ordinary starvation still kills. Death uses the same custody, household, office, role and observed-social consequences as other Person deaths.

Contact is valid only at explicitly configured encounter nodes, with both opposing Persons physically present. This is a coarse graph-local abstraction, not weapon reach or battlefield geometry. Each person performs at most one outgoing strike action at once; multiple actual people may target one opponent. Base strike work15min, scaled by current physiology and injury. One completed valid strike causes one injury. All simultaneously due valid strikes are evaluated against the same pre-damage state and accumulated before application. No RNG hit roll, unit HP, numerical attack bonus, morale or magic protection. Target departure, incapacity, death or service expiry invalidates an unfinished strike. Arrivals occur before contact resolution at a shared timestamp; completed hits resolve before raid settlement. Incapacitation cancels the victim's ongoing work/queue/combat. No attacks in transit.

Theft is a distinct5min base-work action per integer food quantum. It requires actual stock presence, free carrying space and no capable opposing defender at that node at start and completion. A defender arriving interrupts a raid rather than creating a protection score. Quantity/age are conserved. Title remains with the Commons; custody changes to the raider. This models unlawful taking, not new lawful property rights. Raiders cannot use ordinary communal withdrawal on target stock. Stolen cargo may be consumed physically under existing communal-cargo ceiling; this is recorded separately from theft and does not legitimize title. No automatic recovery or redistribution after death. Per-raider controlled objective limits this raid to10 quanta, then return to origin with cargo; no repeated infinite haul loop.

Raider purpose and target are controlled initial conditions locally given to existing Persons, not a theory of criminal motivation. Voluntary defenders must first arrive at muster; one local Protect objective gives existing F11B movement to the target and permission to engage locally observed raiders. No absent member receives it. Neither side gets remote positions, inventory or routes. Known route memories are explicit initial conditions. Immediate needs and care remain in common Person arbitration; contact action completion is persistent but interruptible by critical survival/recovery. Ordinary preferences do not restart an in-progress strike. No separate SoldierAI.

## Controls and gate

Thirty paired seeds per case. Raid/no defense versus same raid with voluntary defense; include no-threat counterparts to measure mobilization without invented benefit. Cases: local, under/over mobilization, distant/late defense, capacity bottleneck, hunger, civilian congestion, paths on/off, physically built crossing and household/cultivation opportunity costs. Keep threat timing, number, stock and physics identical within pairs. Register exact fixture parameters in code/outputs before final panel. Do not adjust contact coefficients based on victory/defeat.

Report gross stock theft, remaining stock and consumed/spoiled quantities separately; a cumulative theft difference alone is not proof of nourishment or recovered goods. Record contacts, injuries, incapacity/death, assembly, arrival, food consumption, queues, labor, care, starvation and household effects with fixed starting cohorts. No monetized net welfare score. Failure and survival-only limitations remain visible.

Pass only with a real undefended loss and at least one full voluntary assembly → contact → smaller physical loss chain, plus timing/geography effects and civilian costs. Validate static action contracts, concurrent damage/theft conservation, own injury forecast, ordinary death, locality, and all historical regressions. No conquest, forced levy, requisition, wages, technology, healing, looting other inventories or compulsory service.
