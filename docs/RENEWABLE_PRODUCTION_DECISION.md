# F3 crop-rights decision — accepted 2026-09-23

The owner selected B: growing crop and harvest belong to the cultivator. Ownership
starts when successful cultivation begins, not at harvest. This is provisional.

The exclusive claim covers the active crop/process only. No land title, communal
farming, open-access harvest, inheritance, seizure or scavenging is authorized.
Dead cultivators retain ownership; inaccessible crops remain recorded. Food remains
personal unless an existing explicit voluntary transfer changes ownership.

Proceed under [the registered model and experiments](FARMING_EXPERIMENT.md).
Earlier unselected alternatives were a decision proposal, not implemented rules.
