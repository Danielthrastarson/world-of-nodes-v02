# F9B — persistent physical local barter

Owner authorized this institution after F9A; money remains unauthorized. Preserve
all prior experiments. Preregistration precedes implementation and measured results.

## Single institutional intervention

Direct F9A barter is the control. Treatment has initially designated market nodes
at the existing settlement nodes A and B (not the corridor). This is a local
institution, not a constructed building or new route. Identical worlds, people,
stocks, physiology, FIFO, capital, farming, paths, priorities and offer-generation
code. No trade-search or buyer-waiting behavior. Six fixtures from F9A, seeds 0–29,
168 hours, direct versus market: 360 runs. Controlled two-person opportunities
remain distinct from the four 50-person F8 worlds.

Posting physically splits/moves the offered quantity into MarketEscrow(offer_id).
Ownership and original age persist. Only escrow settlement or local owner
cancellation can release active collateral. One active offer per proposer remains.
Term is exactly 12 hours; earlier spoilage invalidates exchange and releases waste.
Departure, work and remote death do not revoke deposited standing consent. This
interpretation avoids an implicit death-report channel: a previously authorized
swap may still settle before expiry; payment remains the original owner's property,
including after death. There is no inheritance or appropriation.

Acceptance requires an idle, living local buyer with the exact personal payment.
Seller presence/availability is unnecessary. Both ownership transfers are atomic;
both outputs are loose at the market. No absent seller wake-up or receipt message.
Expiry/cancellation releases collateral locally, never into a remote inventory.
Existing local pickup, eating and construction can use released goods/payment;
there is no new retrieval journey policy. Record retrieval separately from use at
the market, spoilage and property remaining after death.

Policy formation and willingness remain F9A. Escrow is excluded from spendable
inventory. For local reconsideration of one's own offer only, include its collateral
in the surrender-surplus calculation: depositing is not itself loss of ownership or
evidence that promised goods vanished. This is custody accounting, not a new offer
motivation. No remote offer queries enter decisions. Diagnostic counters do not
enter policy or historical hashes.

## Validation and evidence

Before live runs test atomicity, competing buyers, locality, seller departure and
work, no remote notification/cancellation, age/spoilage, reservation exclusion,
load limits, escrow inaccessible to ordinary actions, expired/paid local retrieval,
owner death, and deterministic replay. Direct-arm state/event histories must match
all 180 enabled F9A runs. Hash every pre-existing research artifact before/after.

Report eligible offer evaluations (not independent people), formed/deposited offers,
unique local observer/offer pairs, unique declines with explicit causes, closures,
swaps/ratios/rights/location, latency, traders, escrow stock/time, released/paid
goods remaining, retrieval and local use, death lock-up, production, consumption,
starvation, migration, construction and labor. Explain measurement denominators.
No purposeful exchange travel exists; paired labor differences are not automatically
trade-search costs. Distinguish low offer formation from rejection of actual offers.

Double coincidence requires a trace of mutually relevant possessions and voluntary
release, but no wanted payment; missing inventory or an unattractive ratio alone
does not qualify. Research may inspect preferences for diagnosis, never for policy.
Any unsupported monetary inference stays unestablished. Gate: correct physical local
institution and explainable paired effect. Stop after evidence, regardless of swaps
or welfare; do not adopt further economic mechanisms.

## Locality audit correction (before final evidence)

An added adversarial test found that the inherited global one-open-offer check
could reveal remote settlement when the proposer tried to post elsewhere. Preserve
`research/market/remote-admission-failure.txt`. F9B admission now uses a private
record of the proposer's outstanding consent. A visit to that market can refresh it;
the fixed known 12h deadline can expire it. Remote acceptance does neither. The
formation rule still proposes the same bundles; this corrects institutional
admission, not motivation. A stale commitment may therefore suppress a new posting.
No polling, message, knowledge channel, or automatic journey was introduced.

The initially launched matrix is retained at `research/market/matrix`, revision
76511cc, as pre-repair evidence. Final research reruns the treatment after the fix
and compares physical event histories and metrics, identifying any changed runs.
State hashes differ because the repaired private record is now part of state.
