# F9A — alienable personal goods, local spot barter

Owner authorized voluntary alienation and atomic barter. No money, credit, theft,
inheritance, wages, rental, land/crop/cart-title transfer or communal appropriation.
F8 and its arrival-without-deposit trace remain preserved. Existing policy order
for communal jobs is untouched. This preregistration precedes measured outcomes.

## Institution

An identified owner can post one standing local offer giving a specified existing
batch quantity for a resource/quantity. Posting is consent to those exact terms
until revocation, departure, death, loss/spoilage of the offered goods, or one hour
expiry. The one-hour lifetime is an explicit provisional offer protocol, not a
forecast of travel. No goods are reserved. Acceptance revalidates both inputs before
either moves. Goods held by the owner (including an accessible owned cart), or
owned loose goods at the same node, qualify. Natural/communal/foreign goods do not.

The taker selects a personally owned local payment batch. Both agents must be awake
and idle at settlement; ongoing travel/work cannot be secretly interrupted by the
other party. Both outputs become recipient-owned loose goods at that same node.
This deliberately separates exchange from pickup/carrying and cannot bypass load
limits. Food ancestry/age survives splitting. Failed acceptance changes no goods.
No remote offer observation, matching, transport, or other owner's inventory query.
Owners may revoke or ignore. A standing offer is advance consent, not an order to
perform future labor or guarantee that goods will still be available.

## Bounded autonomous willingness (experimental assumptions)

The institution alone cannot specify preferences. Make this simple rule inspectable:
food enables current/near-term nourishment; wood enables an already-believed useful
cart investment. No speculative resale or utility for stone/waste in this arm.

Food demand is the integer shortfall between current nutrition plus locally owned
edible food and12 nutrition-hours. A food seller protects that shortfall plus the
existing six-quantum personal buffer; only food above it may be offered. Wood demand
is the missing recipe input only if the existing capital evaluator, supplied a
conditional personally owned input, judges the known cart opportunity worthwhile
and survivable. This is a belief-only test, not free construction or new demand.
Wood protected for that plan cannot be sold; other wood has no modeled immediate
use. These are bounded use categories, not prices or an aggregate welfare function.

Offer at most six surplus food quanta for up to the missing wood recipe quantity,
or up to ten surplus wood for the food shortfall. Ratios come from holdings and
needs, not a random pairing or common unit. Acceptance requires a positively useful
receipt and surrender entirely outside protected uses, with exact physical stock.
Both sides' reasoning is logged. Refusal is valid. No attempt to make these
preferences optimal; their limitations must accompany economic conclusions.

Only idle agents without communal jobs may consider barter. Immediate legal eating
and critical sleep retain priority. A local offer/acceptance consumes the same
decision opportunity as existing instantaneous actions. No barter search route is
introduced. Existing movement may bring owners together; exchange-induced changes
in later travel are measured, not mislabeled as purposeful market-search labor.

## Paired design

Seeds0–29, enabled versus disabled in each world;168h. Four unchanged F8 worlds:
complementary2h, symmetric2h, low-cost0.5h, high-cost4h (240 runs). Existing carts
available, paths frozen, same50 people, stocks, production, rights and local memory.
Disabled arms must reproduce their F8 physical/event hashes exactly.

Two additional controlled opportunity fixtures (120 runs) isolate whether the
mechanism can operate when complementary personal holdings exist. Two people, same
graph/capacity/physiology,24 personally owned food quanta at A for agent0 and10 wood
for agent1. Remaining2976 food and490 wood at B; two cultivation units at A. Agent0
has documented t=-1h memories of the route and a100-quantum request at B; live boards
have no preloaded shortage. Memories are stale, conditional evidence, not updates.
Agent1 knows only its starting node. In co-location both start at A with their
goods; in separation agent1 and its wood start at B. No forced encounter or exchange.
These are mechanism fixtures, not directly comparable welfare estimates for50 people.
Initialization allocates existing finite goods; it does not create or move goods
during runtime. The enable/disable comparison changes only barter availability.

## Evidence and gate

Test consent/refusal/revocation, atomic failure, concurrent acceptance, expiry,
spoilage, death, ownership, age, carrying constraints, locality and deterministic
histories before live runs. Research diagnostics may inspect world truth; policy
receives only its observation/memory and visible offers. Keep research out of hashes.

Record offer lifecycle, rejection reasons, exact bundles/ratios, unique/repeat
traders, owner/holder/origin/expiry before and after, subsequent physical carriage,
labor, migration, hunger/death, production/consumption/spoilage, stocks, capital and
missing-input gates. Zero attempted exchange differs from actual rejection.

Double-coincidence evidence requires demonstrated willingness to release the wanted
good plus rejection of what the other actually owns; missing payment alone is not
enough. Unsupported reasons (e.g. too costly to reach when no trade search exists)
are not fabricated. Do not infer money demand, intertemporal contracts or speculative
resale from rules that cannot express them. F9A passes for conserved voluntary local
success/failure with explanatory evidence, not welfare gain. Stop before money or
any additional institution; report what F9B can and cannot conclude.
