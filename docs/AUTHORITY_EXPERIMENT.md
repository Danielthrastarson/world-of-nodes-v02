# F10: embodied delegated authority — registered before implementation

Baseline: 15ac26b. F9 is closed with MONEY NOT CURRENTLY JUSTIFIED. Rare autonomous
large-world offer formation is OPEN ECONOMIC RESEARCH DEBT. No economic policy
changes are authorized. All existing research is covered by prior-artifacts.json.

## Minimal institution

An opt-in polity has a stable ID, an existing sovereign agent, community Commons(0),
and a registry of settlement identities (nodes, not resource information). The polity
has no belief state. Steward offices have their own stable IDs, holder, settlement,
appointment time and appointing sovereign. The appointment is a typed local action:
living sovereign and candidate must share a node; the target must be a registered
settlement. It conveys the assignment identity, never a route or remote state.
It does not change the candidate's action, goal, inventory, or beliefs.

No appointment orders travel, residence or work. Controlled deployments below are
explicit research commands, not autonomous government. Multiple appointments may
coexist, including multiple offices for a person; physical presence limits actual
coverage. Repeating the same holder/settlement appointment is rejected. This adds
no exclusive territorial claim against another polity (only one polity tested).

An appointed office is a persistent historical record. Administration is derived:
valid office, living holder, holder at its assigned settlement. Sovereign death stops
new appointments; existing delegations persist. No succession, automatic replacement,
revocation, salaries, administrative work quota or abstract cost is introduced.
The registry is authoritative institutional state, not global NPC/player knowledge.
Local observation exposes only physically present holders at the observer's node;
true territory and office metrics are server/research queries, never policy inputs.

In authority-enabled worlds, configured crossing sites are physical opportunities,
but are not executable public objectives until locally commissioned. Only a steward
of that site physically present there may issue the typed commission. The site and
recipe must actually exist locally. Commission records identify office, person,
polity, project, location and time; resources/ownership do not change. Existing F6
voluntary donation/building requires that commission. Completed crossing stays
Commons(0), not personally or royally owned. Existing commissions persist through
issuer departure/death: losing current authority does not retroactively revoke a
locally issued objective. No remote wakeup or memory refresh follows commissioning.
Outside this opt-in institution, all historical F6 semantics remain unchanged.

Appointment/commission use the existing instantaneous institutional-action semantics
(like claims); no invented processing duration. Embodied hunger, fatigue, time,
travel and death remain real. Holding office does not prohibit other work at the
settlement. The food/time cost reported is the agent's actual consumption and time,
not a claimed causal increment over a counterfactual civilian.

## Research strata, fixed before outcomes

1. Controlled physical delegation, 72h, 50 existing agents, all start at node 0,
12h nutrition, 4h fatigue, normal hunger/death, capacity 4 except registered bottleneck.
C-X-F graph with 3000 food at F, original age 0, expiry 48h. Autonomous policy disabled
ONLY in these mechanism controls; no food gathering, eating, sleep, teleportation or
physiology intervention. This deliberately exposes dependence on provisioning.
Sovereign 0 appoints steward 9 at C for X; both are initially colocated. Actual travel
is submitted through ordinary FIFO. Cases: local edge 0.5h; distant 8h; bottleneck
edge 2h/capacity 1 with eight earlier travelers (IDs 1–8); death-before-arrival edge
40h; death-after-arrival local case; departure local case with return after 1h residence;
multiple: additionally agent 10 appointed for F and follows two 0.5h edges. Default
second edge 0.5h/capacity 4. The death controls deliberately have no replacement.
All offices issued physically at t=0; no preseeded appointments. Commands stop at death.
Outputs record exact transitions, admission/arrival traces, active hours, gaps, latency,
travel/queue time, consumption, office costs and deaths. These are deterministic
mechanism controls, not independent Monte Carlo evidence of political behavior.

2. Autonomous project comparison, 30 paired seeds 0–29, 72h. 50 agents initially at
X; sovereign 0 appoints agent 1 locally for X in BOTH arms. One arm commissions the
local crossing at t=0; the other leaves it uncommissioned. Then unchanged autonomous
policy controls ALL agents including sovereign and steward; no residence commitment.
C-X 0.25h/cap4; X-F 0.25h/cap0, completed crossing opens cap4. Existing F6 recipe
20 wood +8h work. X has 300 food, F has 2700, all age0/spoil48h. Wood at X is 100
(sufficient, explicit prior-memory control), 5 (scarce, same memory), or 100 with
no prior knowledge (discovery). C board request600. No farming, carts, roads or new
information channel. These initial location/material differences from F6 are labeled
controlled opportunities, not replacements for the preserved F6 worlds.

No policy will appoint or commission spontaneously in F10. Those are explicit
institutional commands, foundation for future ruler input. Participation and all
subsequent movement remain autonomous in stratum 2. Failure to build is valid.

## Checks and gate

Tests cover remote/dead/nonsovereign appointment rejection, atomic traversal,
local commission rights, absence/death loss, sovereign death with surviving office,
multiple offices and derived territory, no candidate memory/action mutation, no
remote observer update, no free resources, persistence of issued commission,
unauthorized construction rejection, disabled historical compatibility, conservation
and FIFO. Count active hours per settlement union, not duplicated office-hours.
Report all appointments, activation/gaps, departure/death, commissions, voluntary
donors/workers, completions, embedded wood/work, consumption, starvation, queues and
state/event hashes. Trace exact authority changes and construction participation.

Pass only if physical delegation and geography change effective administration,
authority enables existing construction without forcing resources/labor, and every
invariant/regression holds. This does not establish coercion, legitimacy emergence,
efficient administration or autonomous appointment motivation. Stop after F10 report;
F11 military/coercive institution has not been authorized here.
