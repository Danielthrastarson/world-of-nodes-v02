# Shared-edge congestion — registered bounded design

Preserve all prior evidence and the default policy. No transport policy, geometry,
physiology, material, ownership, institution or capacity changes. Research worlds
are conditional transport instruments, not proposed NPC behavior.

1. Rehydrate the preserved committed seed-0 trajectory at its native event times,
   verify its complete event hash against the frozen result, and capture each first
   board-bound X arrival. This is state reconstruction, not a new hypothesis test.
   Export full focal state, personal memory/intention, ordered queues and occupancy.
2. Report actual waiting as censored when an agent leaves before admission. Also
   calculate an omniscient FIFO service counterfactual with existing applicants
   remaining eligible and no later applications; this is not an agent forecast.
   Integrate actual hunger/fatigue performance. Report the requested conservative
   budget (time to critical hunger minus immediate unqueued traversal duration),
   plus the distinction that an already admitted traversal is atomic and may cross
   critical hunger. Do not equate those budgets with death or exact admission limits.
3. Conditional micro-fixtures preserve the exact focal state at X. Background
   travelers use actual unprovisioned C-side waiting physiology at that time, with
   no food or physiological improvement. Explicit initial placements and queue
   ages are counterfactual, not simulated teleportation. Keep 50 identities and all
   goods. Background actors submit one trip then wait; they age, fatigue, slow and
   can die. All four directional compositions use the same identities/physiology/
   queue order, changing endpoints only. Test every integer ahead count 0–40 (the
   0,4,...,40 grid included), on both observed arrival cohorts. Stop focal evaluation
   at C arrival, death or first survival/policy interruption away from edge 0;
   preserve sleeping interruptions separately. No autonomous background interpretation.
4. Replay all original nonfocal decisions from each captured X state, with native
   timestamps and original initial occupancy. The focal alone uses its unchanged
   committed diagnostic policy. At 100% verify focal failure time/action/physiology
   against the frozen trace. Reduce the *remaining queued migration demand* at the
   fork to 75/50/25/0%, retaining lowest IDs (floor count); cancel excluded requests
   and suppress their later commands. Do not remove bodies already traversing an
   edge. Thus 0% pending load still has the original in-flight traffic. This avoids
   teleporting occupants or changing the focal's earlier discovery/food history.
   Report exact retained IDs, residual occupancy, and the percentage denominator.
   This conditional experiment does not estimate a whole-world migration restriction.
5. Separately compare 30 paired seeds of meal-aware reactive control with a research
   forecast correction. Normalize the planning agent's own currently observed queue
   membership once in copied forecast evidence, and reuse that evidence consistently
   for hypothetical future traversals. Do not subtract twice on the current leg,
   extrapolate departures, or change counts for queues without evidence of membership.
   Preserve original memory and all policy thresholds. No congestion intervention or
   committed arbitration in this arm. Export lifecycle and full forecast journals.

Direction may be irrelevant for identical service demand in this symmetric shared
capacity model. Test it, do not presume opposing traffic has a special penalty.
Retain failures and distinguish transport competition, planning coherence and the
critical-hunger override from literal physical impossibility of surviving an edge.
