# F13B: office-based scoped command obligations

Owner decision: a Person who voluntarily accepts an institutional office accepts
a bounded duty to attempt valid commands in its scope. Authority, obligation,
execution and successful physical realization remain separate. This replaces
the unresolved F13 authority proposal, not the preserved F12/F13A evidence.

This registration fixes the causal contrasts and institutional boundaries before
the experiment panel. Exact fixture dimensions, timings and initial conditions
must be recorded by `parameters.txt` and frozen in the implementation revision
before the panel. Development smoke runs validate mechanics; they are not used
to select parameters for favorable outcomes. Preserve failures and document any
later correction separately.

## Narrow institution

Only the embodied sovereign's scoped request to **commission an existing public
crossing at the recipient steward's assigned settlement** is executable. It
authorizes no seizure, taxation, compulsory construction, conscription,
requisition or arbitrary private behavior. Candidate acceptance of office duties
is explicit and local. Historical F10 appointment records alone must not be
relabeled proof of voluntary acceptance of newly introduced duties.

The sovereign's command is a typed, immutable information object with issuer,
recipient office, objective, issue time and an explicit validity deadline where
used. Revocation is a separate dated information object. Both use physical
information carriage and local handoff. A carrier may hold the message without
being its addressed office holder. A copy does not confer office, permission to
commission or knowledge of remote conditions.

Issuance cannot modify the distant recipient's beliefs, action, goal or duty.
Receipt permits validation using the recipient's own accepted appointment,
message provenance and locally known facts. Authoritative issuance checks may
validate the issuer's own ability to act; recipient policy must not query remote
sovereign health, current remote projects, or a global revocation table. No forged
messages, succession or office revocation institution is introduced here.

An accepted role obligation enters the common Person policy. Critical survival
and indivisible physical actions remain real; a valid role obligation takes
precedence over ordinary optional activities. It can pause, expire or fail.
Explicit voluntary refusal is recorded separately from an invalid command or an
inability to execute. There is no punishment or general obedience parameter.
Register the exact precedence in implementation documentation and test it.

**Completion of a CommissionCrossing obligation means the lawful local
commission was made.** It does not mean a crossing was constructed. Report the
downstream project's material/labor participation and outcome independently.
Zero material, zero volunteers or an unfinished crossing after commission are
physical/project outcomes, not proof the steward refused the command. The study
must not turn a duty to commission into compelled construction labor.

Revocation affects a recipient only after a legitimate receipt. It cannot undo
already expended work, material or a completed act. Revoking a still-open command
does not silently repeal a separately completed public commission. If that
additional institutional operation is not implemented, label it outside scope.
Preserve revocations received before an older order, so later delivery cannot
resurrect a personally known revoked duty. Duplicates must not create extra
obligations or freshen original issue/receipt times.

## Controlled inputs and information limits

The sovereign's decision to issue is a controlled high-level input. Voluntary
office acceptance is an explicit local act or an explicitly labeled initialized
consent record. A courier purpose may be a controlled, already-accepted
undertaking using the F12 common Person route/survival machinery. This is not
evidence of autonomous messenger recruitment or a Crown entitlement to labor.

Routes are personally known through actual or labeled prior observations. A
delivery objective supplies a fixed rendezvous, not the recipient's current
remote location. No automatic tracking or magically replaced dead couriers.
Relevant unknown locations, missing routes, absent recipients, starvation and
queue interruptions remain possible failures. Neither kinship nor office grants
remote knowledge. Test checkpoint-partition invariance around receipt, expiry
and revocation; a pending wake must not make observation calls create decisions.

## Registered paired panel

Ten cases, two arms (`control`, `order`), seeds 0–29: **600 runs**. Mechanism
cases run 24h; the integrated case runs 72h. The same seed and physical starting
conditions are paired within each case. The control omits the high-level order,
not office acceptance or any unrelated institution. Where the message itself
causes courier work, that cost is part of the total intervention and must not be
misreported as a pure receipt effect. Any research deliberation holds or initial
purposes are explicitly disclosed and identical where the paired question
requires them.

| Case | Controlled contrast and intended diagnostic |
|---|---|
| `local` | Prompt physical delivery to the accepted steward; establish receipt → obligation → Person decision → existing local commission. |
| `distant` | Same institution with greater real transmission distance. Measure latency and failure without tracking the recipient. |
| `late` | Physical arrival occurs after an explicitly communicated deadline. Expiration is distinct from refusal and from stale-but-valid execution. |
| `stale` | Conditions change after issue and before receipt; the still-valid message remains unchanged. Separate recipient local evidence from outdated issuer assumptions. |
| `revocation` | Sovereign issues a second physical message withdrawing the open order. Record execution before arrival, actual receipt and subsequent cessation; no cancellation at send time. |
| `death` | Recipient dies before receipt through ordinary embodied mechanics. Delivery/execution failure does not become refusal; no replacement official appears. |
| `scope` | A transported typed command addresses a settlement/objective outside the accepted office scope. Recipient rejects it as invalid; no prohibited operation is implemented for the test. |
| `resource` | Valid commission with insufficient construction material. Distinguish successful institutional execution from downstream material failure. |
| `refusal` | Controlled personal choice explicitly declines an understood, valid obligation despite a feasible local commission. No punishment; this demonstrates the refusal path, not emergent political psychology. |
| `integrated` | Reuse the F13A alternate-route world with scoped order versus no order, retaining ordinary production, households, transport, authority and security. No favorable retuning. |

No particular number of completions, surviving Persons or constructed crossings
is required. A failed delivery is evidence about communication, not a reason to
add global orders. A late order that does cause construction may have worse
outcomes than early authorization; no balancing is added to repair that result.

## Static and adversarial gate before the live panel

Cover at least:

* Sovereign/candidate colocation and actual candidate consent; an unaccepted
  appointment cannot create the new command duty.
* No command effect at remote issuance; no handoff without living capable local
  parties; courier death neither transfers a message nor notifies a recipient.
* Matching issuer, office holder and exact scope; no authority inferred from
  merely carrying a message or being a citizen.
* Remote truth changes leave identical personal inputs and decisions unchanged.
  Known message expiry differs from unknown revocation.
* Local and relayed receipt, duplicates, stale delivery and out-of-order
  revocation; receiving a revoke before the order prevents revival.
* Critical hunger/fatigue interruption without compulsory obedience; ordinary
  optional work cannot erase a valid duty without an inspectable transition.
* The late barter-selection wrapper does not silently replace the selected
  command step or command-delivery undertaking.
* Local commission changes authorization only; goods and project work do not
  appear at issue or receipt. A preexisting completed commission is not repeated.
* Refusal is explicit choice. Unknown route, expiry, death, unavailable material
  and no volunteers never silently become refusal.
* Irregular external time checkpoints yield the same state and event hashes.
  Journal removal does not change choices; newborn Persons inherit no orders.

Then run the full suite and preserved regression panels. Any changed historical
physical outcome must be isolated and explained before expanding the research.
Old raw results and reports remain immutable. New institutional state is opt-in
where needed to replay historical worlds exactly.

## Required evidence and interpretation

Per run record issue/transmission/receipt counts and times, validity decisions
and reasons, obligations formed and transitions, receipt-to-undertaking delay,
commission completions, failures, refusals, expiry, revocation sent/received,
actions before receipt of revocation, message age and actual courier cost.
Separate undispatched, in-transit, received invalid, paused, failed, refused,
expired and completed states. Do not count every uncompleted order as refusal.

For physical realization record wood committed, construction labor, project
completion time, movement/use, farming, care, food consumption/spoilage, deaths,
starvation-person-hours, queue exposure and security loss. Keep ownership,
civilian opportunity costs and temporal changes visible. More crossings or
throughput do not establish benefit. Initial controlled intent is not autonomous
political initiative. Completion of all subprocesses is not a milestone pass.

The full F13 gate needs a traceable chain from one ruler intent through physical
message carriage, voluntarily accepted office scope, role obligation, Person
arbitration and existing local commission into actual second-order physical
consequences. If the chain breaks, report the first stage and preserve it. Do not
introduce another institution to manufacture completion.

## Reproduction and immutable outputs

Build the registered revision and run:

```sh
cargo build --release --bin command_chain_probe
python3 scripts/run_command_chain.py research/command-chain/NEW_MATRIX 30 --compress-traces
```

Probe contract: `command_chain_probe CASE ARM SEED NEW_OUTDIR`. It emits one-row
`results.csv`, `events.tsv`, `parameters.txt`, and any additional causal artifacts.
At minimum results include `case`, `arm`, `seed`, `audit_ok`, `overtakes`,
`state_hash`, `event_hash`. The runner requires passed audits and zero overtakes,
retains partial failures, records source/binary/registration hashes and creates a
combined `runs.csv`. It never overwrites a previous directory. Compression is
lossless and timestamp-independent; only newly generated traces are replaced.

One-seed development smoke panel:

```sh
python3 scripts/run_command_chain.py research/command-chain/NEW_SMOKE 1
```

This checks evidence capture, not effect size or the final gate. Final reports
must identify the frozen source revision, parameters, preserved negative
findings, regressions, causal traces and scope limitations.

## Frozen physical fixtures and interpretation

The ten-case primary panel is 30 paired seeds per case (600 runs). Mechanism
worlds have four Persons, four nodes and capacity4 on open edges. All start at
the project site1. A local explicit office offer and acceptance occur before
king0 and courier2 physically travel along edge0 to outpost0. The king personally
observes project0 while at1. The order is issued one minute after the nominal
outbound trip; the courier returns through the same road. Short edges take0.25h;
`distant`, `late`, `stale`, `death` and `revocation` use8h nominal edges. Impairment,
sleep, hunger and FIFO may make the return much longer or prevent handoff.

The crossing at1 would open edge1 to source2 at0.25h/capacity4, with a4h nominal
alternative through3. Recipe remains20wood plus8 nominal labor-hours. There are
100wood (resource case5), two cultivation units,24 local food and120 source food;
each Person has6 personally owned carried food. Initial nutrition24h and all
physiological rules remain ordinary. Source food expires48h except stale10h.
The stale case establishes changed physical conditions before receipt; no
unobserved food expectation or autonomous royal motive is inferred.

Both arms carry the **same factual report from the king's actual issue location**.
The ordered arm additionally carries the typed command. On physical contact the
research packet copies the held factual report in that same fixed research
boundary before releasing the held recipient. No report is transmitted remotely.
The control omits only the scoped order. Kernel receipt time is measured exactly;
research releases/controlled refusal happen on fixed one-minute boundaries.
Recipient deliberation is held while physiology continues until packet receipt,
or at20h if no receipt. This is a controlled rendezvous, not a residence policy.

Late deadline is one hour after issuance; ordinary order deadline is the run end.
Death case starts accepted steward at nutrition0 and starvation23h, then ordinary
death mechanics apply. Scope payload asks an unsupported private seizure; no such
action exists. Refusal is an explicitly controlled personal decision after receipt
and before optional execution, with no punishment. Revocation case also sends the
steward and a second carrier to0; order is locally received there, steward may
attempt return to assigned1, withdrawal is sent one hour later with carrier3. No
remote cancellation or tracking is permitted. Deliberation controls and seeded
journeys are identical within each pair.

Integration reuses the F13A alternate world, changing initial office consent to the
new explicitly accepted institution. King0 and controlled courier5 physically
travel1→0; issue at1h, courier returns to steward1. Original F12 report carrier2,
F13A household/crops/raid/security controls and fixed6h steward release remain.
Run72h. All other mechanism runs24h. No parameters are retuned to obtain delivery,
construction, survival or welfare. The smoke results and failed test assertions
are retained separately; primary inference uses the frozen implementation panel.

Natural carrier handoff can fail even after physical arrival. If `late`, `stale`
or `revocation` has no receipt, that run does not test its post-receipt effects.
Separate deterministic contact controls validate those effects without replacing
or hiding the autonomous carrier negative. See the mechanism-control report.
