# F8 — physical complementarity under existing institutions

Registered before implementation/results. F7 and all earlier results remain
unchanged. No production law, autonomous policy, property right, queue rule or
information channel changes in this increment. No money, prices or military code.

## Question and institutional boundary

Can two inhabited locations with complementary productive/resource conditions
generate mutually useful physical flows through the institutions already present?
Research labels A/B do not create settlement agents, home tethers, land titles,
territory, employment or centralized allocation. All 50 people remain members of
the existing Commons(0). Two local boards use the existing local shortage/claim
protocol. No inherited remote knowledge and no preloaded demand: requests arise
from local observation. This is two settlements within one cooperating community,
not two sovereign communities with newly invented access rights.

Personal crops/harvests and carts retain F3/F5 rights. Existing accepted communal
jobs may acquire legal food cargo and deliver it physically. LoadCargo can
explicitly transfer the carrier's own loose food to commons ownership for an
accepted job. It cannot appropriate somebody else's crop/output. There is no
owner-to-owner barter, bilateral offer, lending or reciprocal exchange protocol.
Do not reinterpret zero barter attempts as refusal or double coincidence of wants.
An additional exchange institution requires an owner decision; none is selected by
this study. Existing one-way transfers and migration are legitimate outcomes.

## Fixed population, resources and productive mechanics

168h, seeds0–29. Alternating stable IDs start 25 at A and25 at B. Network A—X—B,
capacity4 per shared-direction edge; baseline nominal2h per edge. Nutrition12h,
fatigue4h, backpack10,48h food spoilage and all other physiology/rights unchanged.
Farming uses F3 base sow1h/grow12h/harvest1h/yield24, cultivator ownership. Existing
F5 carts cost10wood/4h, exclusive owner use, existing extra20 capacity. Wood is finite;
no decorative stone demand is introduced because no current action needs stone.

Total initial food3000, cultivation units50 and wood500 match earlier experiment
scales. Complementary distribution: A food2400/plots40/wood0; B food600/plots10/wood500.
The 4:1 food/land split is an ex-ante asymmetry, not a calibration to induce trade.
Symmetric control redistributes the exact totals: each food1500/plots25/wood250.
Complementarity is a resource-distribution package; its comparison does not identify
the separate causal effects of initial stock, fertile units and wood placement.

50 base plots can theoretically yield 1200 quanta per14 nominal-hour cycle before
impairment, contention, ownership, interruptions and transport. This is productive
capability, not predicted output. The50-person population consumes50 nutrition-hours
per living hour. Measure actual production and allocation, not this arithmetic as
a sustainability claim. Cultivation output accounts explicitly for environmental
input using the existing production ledger. Original food still expires at48h.

## Observational measurements

Research-only journal, excluded from decisions, clocks and world/event hashes.
Verify enabled/disabled journal equality. Integrate exact action durations at kernel
boundaries, not sampled activity labels. Every living millisecond belongs to one
activity; dead time is not disguised as idle labor. Report activity by current place
and starting cohort, alongside mortality. Paired differences can identify changes
in realized allocation; they cannot tell which alternative one particular traveler
would have chosen without a separate counterfactual.

Report production/gathering, food consumption, physical inventory by place/material/
ownership, waste, starvation, local need, boards, plots, capital, path state, resource
flows, population relocation and exploration/queue/sleep churn. A→B goods flow means
a real carrier arrived at B after last visiting A, with those goods still physically
present. Count actual arrival cargo, not departure promises. Retain origin, ownership
and material; waste arriving is not food delivery. Gross repeated journeys are not
unique goods. Node X and in-transit stocks/labor remain separate from settlements.

Distinguish inter-settlement visits from migration: report visits, resident-person-
hours and endpoint location by starting cohort, not an invented permanent home change.
Distinguish personal carriage accompanying movement from communal job delivery and
from bilateral exchange (not modeled). Track explicit ownership transitions for
gathering, pickup and communal loading, separately from unchanged-owner movement.

Stored food and local nutrition deficits are physical quantities, not a surplus
score. At checkpoints, report edible stocks by custody, local population/nutrition,
unmet board demand, and unused/dead-owner productive capacity. Count coexistence of
critical hunger with other people's locally present edible loose food separately
from any assertion that a particular exchange would be mutually beneficial.

## Gate and interpretation

Pass if the model demonstrates and causally explains economic interaction or its
absence under these explicit institutions, with conservation, locality, FIFO,
exclusivity and historical evidence intact. Reciprocal trade, optimal allocation,
universal survival and specialization are not required. Do not claim exported
specialization from production concentration alone. Track the difference between
physical scarcity, capability and allocation; no opportunity-cost variable enters
the kernel.

At completion classify evidence for heterogeneous comparison, double coincidence,
intertemporal exchange, labor valuation, capital access and scarcity signaling.
Separate demonstrated obstructions from questions not testable without an exchange
mechanism. Money is not implemented or presumed to solve any of them.
