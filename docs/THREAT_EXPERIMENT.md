# F11C opening question — concrete threat before combat

F11B's organization/movement gate has passed. F11C begins at threat specification; no combat implementation or protection result is included in F11B. F11A's full benefit gate stays open.

## Smallest proposed threat

Use existing embodied Persons in a controlled hostile group, with an explicitly seeded harmful objective against a named civilian at a settlement. The concrete harm is bodily injury/death to that Person, not a safety score, territorial color or abstract economic loss. The hostile actors start elsewhere and must physically reach the victim through the same graph/FIFO, with real hunger, fatigue and resources. Their harmful intent is an experimental initial condition. It does not establish a general hostility, criminal punishment, revenge-right or diplomacy system.

Use a voluntary unit from F11A/B as the potential defender. It must actually assemble and reach a protective position. A membership list, an issued order, or an absent defender must not protect anyone. A defensive objective is received locally; no remote detection or retargeting. Protection, if any, must follow from the defenders physically engaging/intercepting the actual threatening Persons before they harm the civilian. No global aura, resource duplication, forced enlistment, road priority or cart requisition.

## First physical contract to settle before code

The graph supports nodes and along-edge progress, but has no continuous battlefield footprint. Being in the same settlement cannot silently establish weapon reach. Before a contact mechanic is implemented, register its explicit encounter/contact abstraction, physical duration, interruption, simultaneous-event ordering and causal injury/death effect. Do not retrofit line/column bonuses or random group casualty percentages. The smallest mechanism must resolve individual interactions and ordinary Person death, not subtract an abstract army-strength value. Geometry beyond the implemented contact abstraction must be stated as a limitation.

This is a mechanical research model, not a claim of realistic combat. No weapon industry, siege, morale scalar, prisoners, conquest or coercive economic rights are needed for the first test.

## Paired controls

1. No threat, no mobilization: civilian baseline.
2. Threat, no defenders: establishes that the specific adversary can actually reach and harm the civilian.
3. Same threat with timely voluntary assembly and physical positioning.
4. Same threat with late/partial assembly or a bottleneck: a paper unit cannot grant protection.
5. Same defending presence without a threat: measures the civilian cost of service without crediting invented benefit.

Keep people, starting goods, geography, threat schedule, contact rules and paired seeds fixed except the named intervention. Prespecify rather than tune contact timings to get a defensive victory. Military food and all movement remain shared-world mechanics.

## Evidence required

Record threat observations, local orders, actual contacts, interrupted harmful actions, injuries/deaths with actor/target/time/location/cause, survivors, defender losses, households losing labor/caregivers, information-dependent social responses, food consumption, civilian work displaced, queues and starvation-person-hours. Compare protected civilian harm avoided with actual civilian/defender costs. Do not collapse these into an invented monetary/welfare score.

If no undefended harm occurs, the threat control fails; fix the experiment before claiming protection. If physically present defenders cannot prevent harm, preserve that negative result and identify the causal stage. If they prevent harm while imposing costs, report both. Only demonstrated protection can address F11A's open full gate; an army's existence never does so automatically.

No compulsory service, requisition, wages, new property rights or succession are authorized by this test. Those remain separate owner decisions.
